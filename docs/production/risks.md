# KINETIC — Risk Register

_Ranked by likelihood times impact. Each has an owner, a test that detects it, and the fallback already decided._

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Detect | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The panic window is unfair over real latency.** A player who reacted in time dies anyway because the server disagrees. | High | Fatal | Phase 0 week 5 test: survival rate at 80 ms within 10 points of 0 ms. | Server-side cut validation with a grace window (tech/networking.md). Lengthen the Haul ramp from 0.4 s. As a last resort, a brief "line seated" lockout before force applies. |
| 2 | **Heavy-vs-Light pull diverges between clients.** Two players see different outcomes of the same line. | High | Fatal | Record both clients' positions in the week-5 test; diff. | Server-authoritative outcome with client visual blending. Reduce mass spread (220 to 180 kg) if prediction cannot hold. |
| 3 | **Physics explosions.** Stacked containers, rigid Hang lines, and ragdolls interact badly and launch things at absurd speeds. | High | High | Any object exceeding 60 m/s logs a warning; count per session. | Velocity clamps per tier. Hang lines non-solid to objects (already in 05 section 3.5). Substepping. |
| 4 | **Readability collapse.** With six lines, three blasts, and a collapsing scaffold on screen, players cannot find the line on their body. | Medium | High | "What killed you" log: any "I don't know" answer. Edge-indicator find time in playtests. | Line priority rendering: lines on your body render above everything. Cap on simultaneous blasts. Darken non-threatening lines when one is on you. |
| 5 | **Destruction budget blows the frame.** Hangtown's houses or the Anchor's scaffold cost more than the physics budget allows. | Medium | High | Unreal Insights capture per arena against tech/performance-budgets.md. | Per-arena dynamic caps (prop manifests). Kinematic sway for distant houses. Pre-fractured scripted events instead of simulated ones. |
| 6 | **The Doubler's overspin feels like self-punishment, not risk.** | Medium | Medium | Doubler pick rate and win rate in Phase 1 playtests. | Shorten snap recoil; let the snap impulse be the reward; or cut overspin and keep two lines only (bible/04 open question 5). |
| 7 | **Breakers are either unkillable or useless.** Brace makes them static; height makes them trivial. | Medium | High | Breaker win rate per arena; ring-out share on Breakers. | Tune brace foot-hold (05 section 5.4) and Breaker mantle height. Add rated loads where Breakers underperform. |
| 8 | **Climbers never touch the floor and the trap game never happens.** | Medium | High | Average Climber floor time per round; Rigger set-line trip rate. | Perch limit from 1.5 s down. Updraft and Pulse as anti-air. Longer Rigger lines reach them. |
| 9 | **Mover vs CharacterMovementComponent choice is wrong and costs a rewrite.** | Medium | Medium | Phase 0 week 3: drag model works on the chosen system with prediction. | Decide by week 3 with research in tech/player-physics.md. Keep the drag model behind an interface. |
| 10 | **Tone lands wrong.** The Stall fiction reads as cartoonish, or the lethality reads as cruel. | Low | Medium | External playtest exit interviews on tone. | The bible is written to support either reading (bible/04 open question 3). Adjust Marshal voice and Scrap Card copy first. |
| 11 | **Scope.** Four arenas and ten Faces is a lot for the team size. | High | Medium | Milestone slips over two weeks. | Ship with three arenas (cut the Anchor to Season 1) and six Faces. Nothing in the design depends on count. |
| 12 | **Slack-cable cutting in Hangtown is a griefing tool.** | Medium | Low | Team-kill rate from cut cables. | Restrict to Scrapper harness, or to enemy-occupied structures only. |
