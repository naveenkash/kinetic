# KINETIC — Design Brief

_The originating brief. The world bible (01–04) is built against this and should never contradict it._

- **Genre:** Multiplayer physics-trap arena (FPS / third-person)
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5, with a focus on Chaos Physics and destructibility

## Core concept

A high-mobility, fast-paced arena game with zero firearms. Players eliminate each other using momentum, geometry, and environmental physics. The only weapon is a wrist-mounted **Smart Cable** that tethers any two objects (or players) together, plus a melee **Thermal Knife** for defense.

## The Smart Cable

Players shoot Node A and Node B to create a tether, then activate one of three physics states:

- **Contract.** Violently pulls the two nodes together (tethering a player to a moving train; pulling a heavy crate down onto an enemy).
- **Repel.** Violently pushes the two nodes apart (tethering two explosive barrels to create a localized blast; launching yourself off a wall).
- **Suspend.** Locks the cable length and makes it rigid: tripwires, barricades, pendulum swings.

## Combat and emergent gameplay

No health bars depleted by bullets. Eliminations happen through kinetic impact, environmental hazards, or map ring-outs. Players build rapid, on-the-fly Rube Goldberg traps from load-bearing structures, vehicles, explosives, and gravity. Combat is about outsmarting the opponent's trajectory, not twitch-aiming at their head.

## Counter-play: the Thermal Knife

When a player is tethered to a hazard, they are not instantly killed. They have a 1–2 second window of panic to whip the camera around, locate the glowing tether on their body, and slash it with the Thermal Knife before the kinetic force kills them.

## World-building directives

1. **Lore.** Why are these combatants fighting? Corporate bloodsport, prison break, faction war over physics-defying technology?
2. **Factions / characters.** Archetypes based on mobility and trap style (heavy demolitionists vs. agile trappers, etc.).
3. **Map concepts.** 3–4 arenas that maximize verticality, moving parts, and destructible environments.
4. **Progression.** How the lore ties into character progression, cosmetics, and Smart Cable upgrades.
