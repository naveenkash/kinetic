# HANGTOWN: Signature Plays

_Arena: Hangtown (Vilca's Hang). File purpose: eleven fully specified plays unique to this arena, with positions, class, inputs in 05 section 10 style, timing against the Sway, the Lift, and Cable Day, and counters._
_Sources: docs/bible/03-arenas.md (Hangtown signature plays: Eviction, the Swing, Cut-Loose, Overboard), docs/bible/02-factions-and-archetypes.md (named rigs), docs/bible/05-core-mechanics.md v0.2 and the v0.2.1 rulings (3.2 modes and the Kick, 3.3, 3.4, 4.3, 4.5, 5.3, 5.4, 6.2, 6.4, 6.7, 7.1, 10). Zones, house names, and Lift clock per docs/arenas/hangtown/layout.md and events-and-moving-parts.md. Everything not in the bible is **PROPOSED**._

Conventions. Class masses 90 / 110 / 130 / 130 / 220 kg (Climber / Scrapper / Rigger / Doubler / Breaker). Pull tension T 400 / 400 / 600 / 800 (1,200 overspin) / 1,000 N. Jack launch speed V (the Kick) 18 / 14 / 12 / 14 / 10 m/s, split by inverse mass, reached in 0.3 s; the Kick never fires when either Dog is in a player's Catch Plate (05 section 3.2 interlock), so a Jack against a player is the T push only, ramped over 0.3 s (600 N on 130 kg is a 4.6 m/s² skid). Reel: in Hang, holding Haul shortens the rod at 1.0 m/s and holding Jack lengthens it, bearing the class Hang load; hanging players climb by Reel, and a Breaker's 1,000 N Haul is the only line that lifts a player (a Climber) outright. Rated loads commit to a Breaker, a Doubler at overspin, or a crew whose lines sum to 1,000 N on one axis. Foot-hold 3 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 5 m/s² (the Breaker's unbraced foot-hold is 5 per the v0.2.1 ruling, so an overspin can take an unbraced Breaker off its feet); braced Rigger 9, braced Breaker 20 (static to every line). Thresholds are inclusive. Houses attach at a rated 1,000 N. No line swings a hanging house dangerously on its own (v0.2.1): house swings here come from gravity. Lift clock t is seconds since the car's departure from the bottom (48 s cycle). "Window" is the time from the first tell to the point of no return; the design target is 1.0 to 2.0 s, with the 05 section 4.3 exception for approaches within 5 m, and with the note that a falling player can fire Dogs until the kill volume.

## 1. Eviction

- **Where:** West Row. W2's orange slack segment is at Z 22.5 to 24.0 on its hitch, 3.5 m above the rated eye and out of reach from the roof. Cutter hangs beside it. Target on the nets under W2 (X -31 to -27, Y +5 to +9) or inside W2.
- **Class:** any Cutter. Scrapper best (Cutter −0.05 s, and they are already here).
- **Inputs:** 1. From W2's roof, self-dog A, Dog B the roof at your feet, Jack: the Kick lifts you 4 to 5 m. 2. At the apex fire Hang to the NW main cable above (airborne Dog fire; the rod holds you at Z 23 beside the segment). A Climber instead Perches the hitch. 3. Hold the Cutter on the orange tape for 0.6 s (saw-whine, sparks). 4. The hitch releases and you are left hanging on your rod. W2 (1,200 kg) falls 12 m to its catch line, arriving at about 15 m/s, and swings there at floor Z 2.
- **Timing:** independent of the Sway for the net target. For the inside target, cut when the enemy is in the room, not on the porch.
- **Why it works:** 1,200 kg through anyone upright on the nets; inside, a 12 m fall onto a wood floor arrives at 15 m/s against a 14.4 m/s Stall threshold. Window 2.2 s from whine to net impact; 0.6 s for the occupant before the floor drops, then a 1.6 s fall in which they can Dog the main cable 16 m up and Hang (a Haul will not lift them).
- **Counter:** hear the zip and crouch (the dropped house's floor at Z 2 clears a crouched player). A Cutter swing on the cutter's Catch Plate does not stop the hold (stagger leaves the Cutter live, 05 7.1 v0.2); a line does: any Haul that drags the cutter 0.5 m breaks their 1.5 m reach. From inside, Dog up and Hang.

## 2. Fuse's Remote

- **Where:** W3 (condemned, no catch, demolition charge strapped to its rated hanger eye at Z 19.0, 0.5 m above the ridge). Shooter on W1's roof (Z 19, a level shot) or the ring's west rail, 20 to 22 m away. Target on W3's porch, roof, or inside.
- **Class:** Scrapper (Medium props under 100 kg travel 30% faster; any class can do it slower).
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A a propane cylinder in W1's kitchen cradle (racked, 200 N; a 400 N line frees it). 2. Dog B the charge at W3's eye (Dogs seat in charges without triggering them). 3. Haul. The cylinder breaks free (lurch, 0.2 s), crosses 20 m at 8.9 m/s² plus the Scrapper bonus, and arrives at about 20 m/s. It is an object impact over 6 m/s to the charge: the charge fires (5 m, 2,500 N s) and the cylinder fires with it (4 m, 1,500 N s). The eye blows. W3 and its porch go down the Well.
- **Timing:** fire when the NW cable is at its south apex (t = 6) so W3 is 1.5 m nearer the ring's sightline.
- **Why it works:** the charge's blast Stalls anyone on W3's roof before the house falls; anyone inside falls 26 m to the kill volume in 2.3 s. Window: 0.4 s ramp plus 2 s of flight, the cylinder singing the whole way, then the 0.3 s hiss.
- **Counter:** Jack the cylinder off its path (anyone); cut the Scrapper's line as it passes a bridge; never stand on a condemned porch next to a taped charge, which is what the tape is for.

## 3. The Swing

- **Where:** West Row. Rigger on the W1-W2 bridge's west end. W2 (1,200 kg) beside them, the Hub 33 m away at (0, 0, 30). Targets on the nets anywhere between W2 and the ring's west edge (X -29 to -13) and on the W1 bridge approach.
- **Class:** Rigger (40 m line reaches the Hub; 12 kN Hang load makes it a swing instead of a drop. A Climber's 50 m line reaches but its 4 kN rod slips from the first instant. A Breaker's 20 m line does not reach).
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A W2's east wall at the eave. 2. Dog B the Hub drum. 3. Hang: a 33 m rod from the Hub to W2. 4. Release: the rod stays as a set line (12 s). 5. Floor Slingshot off W2's roof (the Kick) and Hang to the NW main cable at the apex, hanging beside W2's slack segment at Z 23; or let a teammate do it. 6. Hold the Cutter on the segment for 0.6 s. W2's hitch releases. The house is now a 33 m pendulum from the Hub, displaced 30 m from the vertical.
- **Timing:** cut as the NW cable passes its south apex (t = 6 to 7): W2's sway velocity toward the ring adds to the swing.
- **Why it works:** gravity does the work, as the v0.2.1 ruling requires; the Rigger's rod only holds (a Hang never unseats a placed object, 05 section 6.2). W2 falls along an arc toward the Hub's vertical and reaches the nets at about X -13, 19 m/s, heading east and down; everything on the nets along the arc from X -29 to -13 is hit by 1,200 kg. Rod tension at the bottom (about 24 kN) exceeds 12 kN, so the rod slips at 5 m/s and the arc lands longer and lower than the ideal; the house ends skidding under the ring's west edge. Exact landing needs engine verification (**PROPOSED** arc). Window: the 0.6 s whine, then about 2 s of fall.
- **Counter:** cut the Rigger's rod anywhere along its 33 m (it crosses the W1 bridge at head height); get off the nets west of the ring; a Scrapper recalls nothing here, the house is not theirs to stop.

## 4. High Line Overboard

- **Where:** NW High Line, outer section (X -50 to -25), Well on both sides. Breaker with back to the north hand cable; enemy anywhere on the walkway within 20 m, standing on the south half of the 1.2 m deck.
- **Class:** Breaker (the only T that takes a Standard harness off its feet: 1,000 N is 7.7 m/s² on 130 kg against a 5 m/s² foot-hold). A Doubler's 800 N (6.2 m/s²) also works on a Rigger or Doubler; a Rigger's 600 N only on a Climber (6.7 against 3) or a Scrapper (5.5 against 4).
- **Inputs:** 1. Brace (crouch, stationary, 0.3 s), back against the hand cable. 2. Self-dog A. 3. Dog B enemy plate. 4. Jack. No Kick fires against a plate (05 section 3.2 interlock): this is the motor push at 1,000 N, ramped over 0.3 s.
- **Timing:** none against the moving parts. Do it when they come to you.
- **Why it works:** a braced Breaker is static to every line, so the enemy takes the whole push: off their feet at 0.2 s into the ramp, across the 0.6 m of deck beside them in another 0.4 s, then pushed out and down at 7.7 m/s² while falling. Below X -25 there are no nets: 42 m to the kill volume. Against another Breaker it fails: 4.5 m/s² is a skid they walk against.
- **Window:** 0.7 s from seat clank to leaving the deck (the 05 section 4.3 reference case, in spec), then 2.9 s of fall in which the enemy can Dog the walkway or the main cable and Hang.
- **Counter:** cut the line (it is in front of you if it seated in your chest); Hang to the main cable while falling and Reel up at 1 m/s (Climber retether 0.4 s makes this routine; a Breaker's 0.9 s recall usually does not). Never share a High Line with a Breaker. A Climber who Jacks first gets only a 400 N push against a braced Breaker, which does nothing to either of them.

## 5. The Counterweight Gap

- **Where:** Market Ring east rail (X +10) and the Counterweight Gap (X +10 to +13.4). Counterweight passing Z 13 to 19 at Lift clock t = 7 to 10.5 (descending) and t = 31 to 34.5 (ascending), 2 s guide hum before each.
- **Class:** any for the shove; Breaker for the drop.
- **The shove (class-dependent):** 1. Stand with the NE corner post at your back (something solid behind you, per Overboard in 05 section 10). 2. Self-dog A. 3. Dog B an enemy standing within 1.5 m of the east rail. 4. Jack. No Kick against a plate: the push is T, ramped over 0.3 s, and it works only where T over the enemy's mass beats their foot-hold: a Breaker (1,000 N) on anyone but a Breaker; a Doubler (800 N) on a Climber, Scrapper, Rigger, or Doubler; a Rigger (600 N) on a Climber or Scrapper; a Scrapper or Climber (400 N) on a Climber only. Anything else is a skid the enemy walks against. 5. Time it so the counterweight's top face is at Z 12 to 13 when the enemy reaches X +13.4: a Rigger pushed at 7.7 m/s² leaves the rail, crosses the 3.4 m Gap in 0.94 s at 7.2 m/s, and has fallen 4.3 m by then at 9.2 m/s down. Relative speed against the counterweight (rising or falling at 1.6 m/s) is 10.5 to 13 m/s: above the 9 m/s kinematic Stall. Window 1.2 s from seat clank to contact plus the 2 s guide hum: in spec. **PROPOSED:** paint a 1.5 m hazard band on the deck inside the east rail so the choice to stand there is readable.
- **Drop the Weight (Breaker, or a crew):** 1. Brace on the ring's east half. 2. Dog A the counterweight as it enters the Gap. 3. Self-dog B. 4. Haul at 1,000 N (or two Riggers plus anyone, lines summing to 1,000 N on one axis, 05 v0.2.2): the counterweight is a rated attachment (05 section 6.7) and at 1,000 N it leaves its guides after a 1.0 s groan, falling as a 2,400 kg simulated rated load (**PROPOSED** mass). From Z 16 it reaches the sole plate in 1.7 s and crushes anything in its footprint on the nets. The Lift car, unbalanced, runs down to the bottom landing under its brake at 4 m/s and stays there for the round (**PROPOSED**): the east honest route is gone for everyone, including the Breaker.
- **Counter:** stay 2 m off the east rail during the hum; nothing on the nets at (14, 0) during a Breaker's groan; cut the Breaker's line on the counterweight in the 1.0 s groan if you can reach it.

## 6. The Lantern (Guillotine)

- **Where:** W1's roof (Z 19) and the W1 bridge's west end below it. One propane cylinder from W1's kitchen cradle. The NW main cable 11 m above the roof.
- **Class:** Rigger (two set lines at 12 s; the only class that can afford to leave this hanging while it does something else).
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A the cylinder (racked, 200 N). 2. Dog B the NW main cable above the bridge end. 3. Haul: 600 N against a 441 N cylinder lifts it at 3.5 m/s² (or Hang first and Reel it up at 1.0 m/s; the Reel also lifts a 200 kg water barrel from the ring, well inside a Rigger's 12 kN Hang load). 4. At 4.5 m above the bridge deck (Z 20.5), switch to Hang and release: the cylinder hangs on a set line, lit Bone-white, over the bridge end. 5. Stand anywhere with a view of the bridge. 6. When the enemy is under it, recall your own set line (05 section 3.6: owners recall set lines early; Rigger recall 0.6 s) or cut it from W1's roof edge. Gravity drops the cylinder 4.5 m onto the deck or the enemy at 9.4 m/s: over the 8 m/s trigger.
- **Timing:** set during the NW cable's north apex (t = 0) so the cylinder hangs 1.5 m north of the bridge centerline and swings in over it at t = 6 as the cable comes south.
- **Why it works:** blast radius 4 m, Stall within 2 m, flip to 4 m, on a 1.2 m wide bridge with the Well on both sides for the W2-W3 span and nets for the W1 span. Window 1.0 s fall plus the 0.3 s hiss. The barrel variant (200 kg, 9.4 m/s, 1,900 N s) Stalls on impact without a blast and makes no hiss: window 1.0 s, the floor of the spec. This is 05 section 10's Guillotine as ruled in v0.2.2: recall or cut your own set line, gravity drops the load.
- **Counter:** look up (a Bone set line with a cylinder on it over a bridge is not subtle); a Climber Perches W1's wall and cuts the set line early; cross at a sprint, the cylinder lands behind you; Jack the falling cylinder away (0.3 s, difficult).

## 7. The Verger's Separation

- **Where:** Climber perched on the Hub drum (0, 0, 31). Enemies arriving on the Rim Pendulum: 25 m rods from the NW or SW main cable, swinging in from the West Rim and reaching the Hub rail at 0 m/s, 30 m above the nets (a 30 m fall onto nets is 24.3 m/s against a 24 m/s Stall).
- **Class:** Climber (perch 1.5 s; Dogs fired mid-air at full momentum; 50 m line covers the whole arc).
- **Inputs:** 1. Perch on the drum as the swinger passes the bottom of their arc (2.5 s out). 2. Dog A the swinger's plate (140 m/s Dog). 3. Dog B the Hub drum, or a second swinger on the other cable. 4. Jack (0.2 s switch). No Kick against a plate: the push is 400 N, 3.1 m/s² on a 130 kg rigger, 4.4 on a Climber. On a grounded rigger that is a skid. On a swinger it is the whole story: airborne players take the full force, and 3.1 m/s² for the last 2 s of the arc moves the arrival point 6 m sideways. Between two swingers the push splits and separates them 6 m each. "Separate. Then rise."
- **Timing:** fire at 2 s before their apex. The whole chain fits inside the 1.5 s perch with 0.3 s to spare.
- **Why it works:** a Rim Pendulum arrival is a release onto the Hub rail at the apex, by habit. Displaced 6 m, the apex is over air. A swinger who releases falls 30 m onto the nets (Stall by 0.3 m/s) or, displaced outward past X ±30, into the Well. A swinger who does not release swings back, hanging, and now has to Reel 25 m up a cable at 1 m/s under a Climber who can do this again.
- **Counter:** do not release at the apex if a line is on your plate; stay on the rod and Reel in (the rod holds, the push only deflects); cut the Climber's line (it is in front of you if it seated in your chest); arrive by the Hub Ladder instead.

## 8. Tilt Rack (Cable Day)

- **Where:** Market Ring. Scrapper on the west half by the seam. Enemies who were on the east half at T0 slid to the SE rail at up to 9 m/s, flipped over it (rail is static, flip at 8 m/s), and landed on the nets at (7, -7, 0) staggered. Four cooking cylinders loose and sliding on the 40° deck.
- **Class:** Scrapper (Medium under 100 kg travels 30% faster; instant recall for a missed line).
- **Inputs:** 1. At the siren (T-5), move to the seam on the west side and face the SE rail. 2. At T+4, Dog A a sliding cylinder. 3. Dog B the flipped enemy on the nets 12 m away. 4. Haul. 5. Recall and repeat with the second cylinder.
- **Timing:** T+4 to T+8. The enemies are staggered 0.6 s from the flip and moving at −20% on nets.
- **Why it works:** 400 N on 45 kg is 8.9 m/s², 11.6 with the Scrapper bonus; over 12 m the cylinder arrives at 16.7 m/s, above the 8 m/s trigger (the bonus applies to Haul acceleration, per the v0.2.1 ruling). Blast radius 4 m, Stall within 2 m. Window 1.8 s from seat clank to blast: in spec, and the 5 s siren came before that.
- **Counter:** leave the east half when the siren sounds; from the nets, Jack the cylinder (anyone can, it is loose); cut the Scrapper's line as it crosses the seam.

## 9. Double Tenant (Cable Day)

- **Where:** Market Ring. E1 (1,200 kg) swinging on its 9 s arc through the NE quadrant at Z 15 to 18 (first pass T+5, then every 9 s). Doubler at the NW corner post. Enemy on the west half within 10 m of the NE post.
- **Class:** Doubler (two active lines, overspin).
- **Inputs:** 1. Line 1: Dog A enemy plate. Dog B the NE corner post. Hang. The enemy is a rod's length from the post and cannot leave the NE quadrant. 2. Line 2, as E1 swings away east: Dog C the same enemy's plate. Dog D E1. Haul at 480 N: 3.7 m/s² toward the receding house, a skid on a Rigger, enough to walk them to the end of their rod at the ring's NE edge, in E1's return path. 3. Release line 1 (line 2 restores to 800 N over 0.3 s) and hold for overspin.
- **Timing:** fire line 2 at E1's east apex (T+9.5, then every 9 s). Release line 1 at apex plus 2.5 s. The overspin octave jump sounds at apex plus 3.5 s and the snap lands at apex plus 4.3 s, 0.2 s before E1 re-enters the quadrant.
- **Why it works:** two lines on one plate cost 1.3 s of Cutter work to clear, while the Doubler's timer runs 1.8 s and a house is coming. If the enemy cuts the rod first, the snap at full 1,200 N delivers 1,500 N s: Stall. If they cut the Haul line first, they are still roped to the post in E1's path: 1,200 kg at 6 m/s. The Doubler pays either way: launched 10 m/s backward into the west half's stalls (a 130 kg body at 10 m/s breaks a stall at 300 N s) and staggered 0.8 s.
- **Counter:** cut line 2 (the one that will snap) and then crouch under E1 (its floor passes about 0.5 m above a crouched player at the ring's edge, **PROPOSED** clearance to verify); or Hang yourself to the deck and let the snap's 1,500 N s hit a player who is rigid to a 20 m platform (impulse still applies; this needs a ruling on whether a Hang anchor shares an impulse; flagged).

## 10. Pull the Pin

- **Where:** West Rim, SW gate. The SW anchor pin in the rim face 1 m below the deck, yellow-and-black taped. Enemies on the SW High Line or in W4, W5, W6.
- **Class:** Breaker (1,000 N meets the rated threshold, inclusive); a Doubler at full overspin on a single line; or a crew whose lines sum to 1,000 N on one axis (two Riggers plus anyone, all Hauling the pin from the same gate, 05 v0.2.2). A Guild crew can pull a pin, and a house, without a Breaker.
- **Inputs:** 1. Brace at the gate. 2. Dog A the SW anchor pin. 3. Self-dog B. 4. Haul. 0.4 s ramp, 0.2 s lurch, 1.0 s structural groan with dust, then the authored consequence: the SW main cable releases; the SW High Line sheds its outer 25 m into the Well and dangles its inner 25 m from the Hub; W4 and W5 drop 12 m to their catches; W6 goes down the Well; the Hub tilts 5°; the ring's west half tilts 10° south-west.
- **Timing:** 1.6 s from Haul to first fall. Do it while the enemy crew is crossing the SW line from Spawn A, 10 to 15 s after "live".
- **Why it works:** anyone on the outer SW line falls 42 m; anyone inside W4 or W5 hits a wood floor at 15 m/s (threshold 14.4). The Breaker has spent their opening on the map's biggest lever and should expect the rest of the round to be fought over a tilted ring.
- **Note:** pulling the NE pin instead fires Cable Day early; the scheduled event then fires on the SE cable and the whole east half goes down the Well (see events-and-moving-parts.md).
- **Counter:** a Breaker braced at a rim gate with a line into the rock is the tell; leave the SW line and W4 to W6 within 1.6 s; the Rigger on the line Hangs to the main cable's inner section, which survives.

## 11. Burner Jet

- **Where:** Market Ring east half, north arc. The gas manifold at (4, 8, 16), fixed world geometry, outlet facing east along the deck toward the Counterweight Gap. Enemies on the east half's east third.
- **Class:** Doubler on a single line (800 N) or Breaker (1,000 N). The vent needs a Jack over 600 N applied to the manifold; a Rigger's 600 N is not over, and a Doubler running two lines has 480 N on each.
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A the manifold. 2. Dog B E1's porch post, 12 m east. 3. Jack (the Kick does nothing to fixed geometry; the push is what the manifold reads). It vents: a 6 m jet east at 15 m/s for 3 s (05 section 6.4), authored as 18 m/s² on a 100 kg reference body (**PROPOSED** value) and felt as a_ref × 100 / m (05 section 6.6): Climber 20 m/s², Scrapper 16.4, Rigger and Doubler 13.8, Breaker 8.2. It moves players and loose objects only: the cooking cylinders stay in their rack.
- **Timing:** fire 1.5 s before the counterweight's top face reaches Z 19, which is Lift clock t = 5.5 (descending) or t = 30 (ascending). The jet's 1 s force-volume tell overlaps the counterweight's 2 s hum.
- **Why it works:** every class is over its foot-hold in the jet, a braced Rigger (9) included; only a braced Breaker (20 against 8.2) holds. A Standard harness leaves the east rail at about 9 m/s after 2 m of jet and meets the counterweight (kinematic, 9 m/s Stall, relative speed higher once they are falling) in the Gap; a Climber at 20 m/s² is faster. Window: the 1 s force-volume tell plus 0.7 s in the jet. Without the counterweight they fall 16 m onto the nets, flipped, which sets up play 8.
- **Counter:** brace if you are a Breaker; Hang yourself to the deck (the jet pushes, the rod holds); stand off the manifold's axis, the jet is 6 m long and narrow; cut nothing, the vent is already open.
