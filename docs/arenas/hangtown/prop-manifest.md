# HANGTOWN: Prop Manifest

_Arena: Hangtown (Vilca's Hang). File purpose: complete inventory of placed objects by zone, placement state, orientation, and the prep-crew reason; tier totals; dynamic budget; arena-unique mini-specs._
_Sources: docs/bible/03-arenas.md (map rules 1, 2, 7), docs/bible/05-core-mechanics.md (6.1 tiers, 6.2 break-free, 6.4 volatiles, 6.5 destructibles). Zone names and coordinates are from docs/arenas/hangtown/layout.md. Everything not in the bible is **PROPOSED**._

## Who staged this site

Two hands placed everything here. The **residents** left their homes as they were when the Office evacuated them at 06:00 on match day: stalls, laundry, cooking gas, roof tanks, carts. The Accord forbids the Office from removing residents' property on a contested claim. The **Office prep crew** added the match lights, the Hub pendant, one Stall Gel pad, the pre-rigged catch lines under every non-condemned house, and the structural tape. **HTR's advance survey crew** was here before the claim was contested and left condemnation notices, paint stencils, and two staged demolition charges on the houses tagged for the first cut. Every hazard belongs to one of these three.

States per 05 section 6.2 v0.2: loose (0 N), racked (200 N, anyone), stacked (500 N, Rigger and up), bolted or chained (800 N, Doubler and Breaker), rated (1,000 N, Breaker or Doubler overspin). All thresholds are inclusive. Hanging houses attach at a rated 1,000 N: only a Breaker's line, a Doubler at overspin, a crew whose lines sum to 1,000 N on one axis (two Riggers plus anyone, 05 v0.2.2), or a cut slack cable drops one. The same three apply to the anchor pins and the counterweight. Kinematic and static items are marked as such.

## Dynamic budget check

At round start nothing simulates. Houses, bridges, walkways, the ring, and the nets sway kinematically. Props sit asleep in their placed states. A body wakes when a line seats in it, an impact crosses its break-free, or its structure changes state.

| Class | Recommended cap (simultaneous simulating) | Worst authored case | Headroom |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy | **10** (houses at most 4, per 03's engine note) | Cable Day: E1, E2, E3 swinging (3) plus the marker nudged (1) (roof tanks are fixed and rupture in place) = 4 | 6 |
| Medium | **36** | Cable Day east half spill: 4 stalls, 4 cylinders, plus a Scrapper fight in the West Row: 2 barrels, 1 cart, 2 cylinders = 13; a second house swing adds 2 | 21 |
| Light | **120** | laundry, chairs, buckets, notices and debris near two fights: about 60 | 60 |

Rules (**PROPOSED**): a fifth house asked to wake forces the oldest awake house back to kinematic sway if it is at rest and more than 20 m from any player; if none qualifies, the request waits. Light props sleep after 2 s at rest and despawn fragments at 10 s (05 section 6.5). Houses farther than 60 m from every player drop to kinematic sway regardless. Second Cable (sudden death) removes the nets and terraces by scripted swap, not simulation.

## Arena-unique objects

**Hatch cover plate.** 4.2 m diameter, 20 mm steel disc, 1,100 kg, Heavy. Sits in greased runners (friction 0.05) on the west half frame over the ring's 4 m central hatch (the Eye), bolted at 800 N (Breaker, or a Doubler's single line). Once the bolts shear, the same line keeps it sliding on its runners (about 550 N). Yellow-and-black taped ring, "OFFICE OF THE LAY: KEEP CLOSED" stencil. Physics: when pulled it slides, tips, and falls through the Eye to the nets 16 m below (17.7 m/s): anything under it on the nets is Stalled by impulse (1,100 kg at any speed above 1.1 m/s).
