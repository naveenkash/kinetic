# HANGTOWN: Overview

_Arena: Hangtown (Vilca's Hang). File purpose: story, feel, thesis, format, footprint, tiers, round expectations._
_Sources: docs/bible/03-arenas.md (Hangtown section, map rules 1 to 8), docs/bible/05-core-mechanics.md (sections 2, 4.5, 6, 7, 9, 11). Everything not in those files is marked **PROPOSED**._

## Story

Collar Three split when it landed. The two halves sit 400 m apart across a ravine in the Cordillera. During the Ribbon Wars people with nowhere else to go strung cable between the halves and built on it. Sixty years later three thousand people live in houses that hang. They call themselves Hangers. The district is called Vilca's Hang after the woman who strung the first cable. Her grandson, Jesús "Fuse" Vilca, leads the Hangtown crew.

Halvard-Teague Reclamation holds a demolition claim on both Collar halves. The Lay Guild and the Hangtown Crews contest it. On match days the Office evacuates the Hang, puts up floodlight masts on the rims, and the residents watch their homes get used as weapons on LayCast.

The arena is the narrowest strung section of the district, where the two Collar rims come within 100 m of each other (**PROPOSED** scale resolution). The rest of the Hang runs north and south out of the playable area as a swaying vista, lit, evacuated, and unreachable.

## Who contests the claim

Hangers (Scrapper harness) defend the Hang. HTR (Breaker) wants it cut. The Guild (Rigger) contests on safety grounds and sides with the Hangers in the seasonal narrative. Ascendants (Climber) and the Fray (Doubler) fight here for the broadcast numbers; Hangtown is the second most-watched arena after the Anchor.

A Hanger win in the seasonal Claim keeps the district standing. An HTR win removes a quarter of it next season: cables left hanging empty.

## What the player should feel

Guilt, then glee, then vertigo. The first time a player holds a Cutter against an orange-taped cable for 0.6 s and a kitchen falls out from under an enemy, it should feel like a transgression. By the third round it feels like Hangtown. Under every step is sixty meters of mist and the sound of Hooks. There is almost no floor.

## Thesis

**Two hanging houses swung into each other is the map's thesis statement: in Hangtown the structure is the weapon, the Cutter is a demolition tool, and the Well is under nearly every square meter.**

## Format

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Target modes | Scrap (primary), Claim (primary). Haul not supported: the 2,000 kg spool would spend the match in the ravine, since there is no continuous floor between any two truck positions. |
| Player count | 3v3 at launch. 3v3v3 is a post-launch variant (03); if added, the third crew (Rose tint, 05 section 11a) spawns at the Lift headframe (**PROPOSED**). |
| Dominant class | Scrapper (Cutter on slack cables, Quick Hands on laundry, stalls, and cylinders). Climber second (perches on cable, 50 m line covers rim to Hub). |
| Weakest class | Breaker. Honest routes exist but are slow and exposed. A Breaker in Hangtown is a 220 kg load looking for an edge. |
| Dog advice | Spike. Hangtown is timber and cable. Mag Dogs seat only on the Lift, the counterweight, the Hub, and the anchor pins. |

## Tiers

Heights are meters above the lowest playable floor (the Low Hang drip nets, 0 m).

| Tier | Zones | Height |
|---|---|---|
| Low Hang | Drip Nets, Garden Terraces, Low Shanties, West Face, Lift bottom landing | 0 to 6 m |
| Mid Hang | West Row and East Row house floors, Market Ring deck, Lift Gate landing | 14 m (houses), 15 m (Lift Gate), 16 m (market deck), roofs to 19 m |
| Upper Hang | the four High Lines, the Hub, the headframe ramp | 30 m |
| Collar rims | West Rim (Spawn A), East Rim (Spawn B), solid ground | 32 m |
| Lift headframe | top platform | 38 m |

Rule 3 check: Low to Mid is 14 m, Mid to Upper is 14 to 16 m. Each gap is one Floor Slingshot (self-dog, Dog B the floor at your feet, Jack) for a Climber (30 m of vertical gain at V = 18 m/s) or a Doubler (27 m), and one Slingshot plus a mantle for a Scrapper (15.8 m) or Rigger (13.8 m). A Breaker (9.4 m) takes the honest routes: ladders and the Lift. A Haul cannot lift any player (pull tension is under body weight in 05 v0.2); the one exception is a Breaker's 1,000 N Haul on a Climber. Climbing is done by Reel (05 section 3.2, v0.2.2): in Hang, holding Haul shortens the rod at 1.0 m/s bearing the class Hang load, so any hanging player, Breaker included, climbs a line at 1 m/s. Routes are specified in layout.md.

## Round-length expectations

| Mode | Bible rule | Hangtown expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Scrap | 2:30 rounds, best of 5, event at 1:15 remaining | **PROPOSED** average round 75 to 85 s, the shortest of the four arenas. Ring-outs will run high. Launch target under 35% ring-out share; the lever is the Drip Nets (×2.0 Stall multiplier) and their footprint. |
| Claim | 8:00, first to 100 points | Marker on the Market Ring. Cable Day fires at 4:00 remaining (05 section 9.2) and tilts half the marker's footprint for the second half of the match; expect 5 to 7 minutes. |
| Sudden death (Scrap tie) | arena-specific shrink | **PROPOSED** "Second Cable": the south-west main cable gives and the Low Hang drops away over 20 s. See events-and-moving-parts.md. |

## The one thing to get right

The slack cables. Orange tape, visible fray, a 0.6 s hold with the Cutter, a saw-whine and sparks during the hold, then a twang and a house gone. If that reads from 30 m away and feels wrong the first time, Hangtown works.
