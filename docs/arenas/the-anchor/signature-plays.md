# THE ANCHOR: Signature Plays

_Arena: The Anchor. File purpose: twelve plays unique to this arena, with positions, class, inputs in 05 section 10 style, timing against the cycles, the number that makes each lethal, and the counter._
_Sources: docs/bible/03-arenas.md (The Anchor signature plays), docs/bible/05-core-mechanics.md v0.2.2 (3.2 including Reel and the Kick interlock, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 5.3, 5.4, 6.2a, 6.4, 6.6, 7.1, 10). Inventions are marked **PROPOSED**. Zone names and heights are from layout.md; cycle times and force-volume numbers from events-and-moving-parts.md._

## Conventions used in every play

- **The Kick** (05 section 3.2): every Jack from a self-dog or between objects and geometry opens with the Kick, the 0.3 s capacitor launch to the class speed V of 18 / 14 / 12 / 14 / 10 m/s (Climber / Scrapper / Rigger / Doubler / Breaker), split by inverse mass, followed by the motor push at T to class line length. A static or anchored end takes none of it; the free end takes all of V. Where an input says "Jack (the Kick: 18 m/s)" that is the launch.
- **The interlock:** the Kick never fires when either Dog is in a player's Catch Plate. A Jack against a player is the motor push at T only, ramped over 0.3 s: 400 to 1,000 N, which on a 130 kg body is 3.1 to 7.7 m/s2. Every Overboard below is a push of a few m/s2 over one to two seconds, inside the panic window.
- **Reel** (05 section 3.2): in Hang, holding Haul shortens the rod at 1.0 m/s, holding Jack lengthens it, bearing the class Hang load. Hoists are Reels. A hanging player climbs by Reel.
- Pull tension 400 to 1,000 N. Hang loads 4 to 12 kN. Foot-holds 3 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 5 m/s2 (Climber / Scrapper / Rigger / Doubler / Breaker); braced Rigger 9, braced Breaker 20. Force volumes are felt at a_ref times 100 over mass.
- Rated loads: a Breaker alone, a Doubler at overspin, or any lines summing to 1,000 N on one axis.

## 1. The Ascension

- **Where:** L4 or L5 inner edge, any quadrant, to the Crown lip.
- **Class:** Climber. (Doubler from L5 in calm; every class on the full Breath, see events file.)
- **Inputs:** 1. Self-dog A. 2. Dog B the deck plank under your heels. 3. Jack (the Kick: 18 m/s), aiming 10 degrees inward of vertical. 4. Perch the lip as you pass it. 5. Drop onto the Crown. If you are passing the lip above 12 m/s: 4a. Dog B the Crown surface, Hang, hold Haul to Reel down.
- **Timing:** From L5, any time: you pass the lip at 9 m/s. From L4, launch between 29.5 and 31 s of the Breath cycle, while it builds; you arrive at 8 to 12 m/s as it goes full. At full the Climber feels 15.6 m/s2 of lift and passes the lip at 28 m/s.
- **Why it works:** 18 m/s of Kick plus 4.4 m/s2 of push is 30 m of rise; L5 to the lip is 22 m.
- **Counter:** A Haul on the Climber's plate mid-flight from the Ring or the Crown. Time the Ring: it severs the Jack line's first 4 m if it is overhead. Or stand at the lip and Jack them as they Perch: no Kick against a player, but 600 N on 90 kg is 6.7 m/s2 off a 1.5 s Perch.

## 2. The Held Line

- **Where:** L3 inner edge, 1 m from the Well mouth, facing outward.
- **Class:** Breaker.
- **Inputs:** 1. Brace (crouch 0.3 s). 2. Self-dog A. 3. Dog B an enemy's plate within 20 m on the same deck. 4. Hang. 5. Wait for the Pulse. 6. At 1.0 s, cut your own line.
- **Timing:** Set the Hang at the Marshal's "two." The shove (0 to 0.4 s) lifts the enemy and swings them outward on the rod; the inhale (0.4 to 1.0 s) swings the airborne body back inward past you over the Well mouth. Cut at 1.0 s, when they are over the mouth at about 5 m/s inward.
- **Why it works:** The Scaffold shove is 11 to 16 m/s2 reference; a braced Breaker feels 7.3 against a foot-hold of 20 and is the only pivot on the deck that does not move. The snap drops them 38 m.
- **Counter:** Cut the line before "zero" (chest or shoulder seat at close range: 0.6 s). Or Hang yourself to the Stump face first; then the Pulse holds you and the Breaker's rod does nothing.

## 3. Slab Drop (the Anchor Drop)

- **Where:** North or south crane slab at r 13, Z 54, from L5 or the Ring. Enemy anywhere within Hang reach of the slab.
- **Class:** Two players: any class Hangs, and a Breaker Hauls, or a Doubler at overspin, or a Rigger and a Scrapper Hauling together (600 plus 400 N on one axis).
- **Inputs:** Hanger: 1. Dog A enemy plate. 2. Dog B the slab. 3. Hang. Puller(s): 4. Dog A the slab. 5. Dog B self or the deck. 6. Haul.
- **Timing:** 1,000 N on the rated slab fires the authored release: 1.0 s brake squeal, then free fall. Passes L5 at 1.9 s, the kill volume at 3.8 s. The enemy's window is the Hang seat plus the squeal.
- **Why it works:** 4,000 kg on a rigid rod. No Hang load, brace, or foot-hold holds against it. The enemy goes where the slab goes.
- **Counter:** Cut during the squeal. Or push the Hanger off the Ring with a Haul before the pull lands. The Ring severs the Hanger's rod if it sweeps through it; watch its shadow.

## 4. The Buddy Line

- **Where:** Any Scaffold deck before a Pulse.
- **Class:** Rigger (support) with a Breaker or Rigger teammate; works for any teammate, weaker with a light one.
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A teammate's back plate. 2. Dog B the deck planks outward of them, 2 m. 3. Hang. 4. Release (set line, 12 s). Repeat for the second teammate. Teammate: 5. Self-dog A. 6. Dog B enemy plate, enemy inboard of you near an inner-rail gap. 7. Jack at "zero" (no Kick against a player: the T push only).
- **Timing:** Set both lines at "five." The set lines outlive the Pulse (1.0 s) with 11 s to spare. The push runs from "zero" through the Pulse and for the two seconds after, while the enemy is still off balance.
- **Why it works:** A set line holds 12 kN; the shove loads it at 1.6 kN. The anchored teammate is the solid end: a Breaker's 1,000 N on a 130 kg enemy is 7.7 m/s2 inward, 4 m in 1.0 s at 7.9 m/s, through a 2 m rail gap and across the 4 m Well mouth. A Rigger's 600 N does it in 1.3 s. A Climber's 400 N (3.1 m/s2) only skids a Rigger and should pick a lighter target.
- **Counter:** Cut (1 to 2 s window, chest seat). Cut the set line; it is dim until you are within 5 m, so look for the Rigger, not the line.

## 5. The Gate Gallows

- **Where:** Crown south lip, against a climber on the Crown ladder (Z 38 to 60). Anchor: the south boom tip at (0, -13, 56).
- **Class:** Rigger (12 kN Hang load, 40 m line). Any class can do it; a Climber's 4 kN Hang still carries a 2.2 kN Breaker.
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A the boom tip. 2. Dog B the climber's back plate at Z 48 to 52. 3. Hang. 4. Hold Haul to Reel: the rod shortens at 1 m/s and lifts them off the rungs toward the tip. 5. When they hang under the tip over the Well mouth, release (set line, 12 s) or cut.
- **Timing:** 6 to 8 s of Reel. The next Pulse's inhale swings the hung body into the Stump face; cut at the whip-crack.
- **Why it works:** The ladder is the honest route, and a rigger on it shows a back plate to the whole south half of the map. Hoisted players cannot move. The tip is over the Well mouth; the drop from it is 56 m.
- **Counter:** The window is long by design. Turn 150 degrees and cut. Or, before climbing, self-dog and Hang to the ladder: a Reel against a rod to static slips instead of lifting. Or take the slower Reel climb from L5 (play 11 counterpart in layout.md) where your chest faces the lip.

## 6. Early Strike

- **Where:** L3 W, the demolition charge on the west clamp at the Stump face. Fire from L3 N or S, across the Well mouth, 12 to 18 m.
- **Class:** Scrapper (Light props travel 30% faster under Haul; instant recall for a second try).
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A a loose paint can or bucket. 2. Dog B the charge. 3. Haul.
- **Timing:** Any time before 1:15 remaining. Best at a Pulse count, when the W deck is full of people Hanging in.
- **Why it works:** 400 N on a 4 kg can is 100 m/s2; it hits the charge well over the 6 m/s trigger. 2,500 N s drops the L3 W deck onto L2 W, Stalls anyone within 2.6 m, flips anyone within 3.8 m. Dogs seat in the charge without triggering it; the can does the work.
- **Counter:** Stand off the W quadrant. Or Dog the charge yourself and Haul it into the Well mouth: it falls and detonates on nothing.

## 7. Pulse Bolt

- **Where:** Any deck, in the five seconds before a Pulse. A loose propane cylinder on the inner half of the deck (pull one from the L3 N rack beforehand: 200 N, anyone).
- **Class:** Scrapper. Anyone can set the cylinder; the Scrapper's instant recall lets them re-aim after the shove.
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A the loose cylinder. 2. Dog B the enemy's plate, enemy outboard of the cylinder. 3. Haul at "one." 4. Let the Pulse do the throwing.
- **Timing:** At "zero" the shove gives a 45 kg cylinder 36 m/s2 outward: 14 m/s in 0.4 s. Your Haul only steers it onto the plate.
- **Why it works:** Contact above 8 m/s detonates; inside 2 m is an authored Stall. The cylinder is the fastest thing on the deck for one second and it is pointed at everyone standing outboard of it.
- **Counter:** Cut the Haul (chest seat, the cylinder is lit). Stand inboard of anything loose before the count ends. Hang in.

## 8. Anchored Overboard

- **Where:** Any Scaffold deck, in the two seconds around a Pulse. After the Collapse: anywhere along the N or S deck toward the open west seams.
- **Class:** Doubler.
- **Inputs:** Line 1: 1. Self-dog A. 2. Dog B the Stump face across the Well mouth. 3. Hang. Line 2: 4. Dog C self. 5. Dog D enemy plate, enemy inboard of you near an inner-rail gap, or between you and an open seam. 6. Jack at "one" and hold for overspin (no Kick against a player: the push is 480 N, 720 N at overspin).
- **Timing:** Line 1 at "three," line 2 at "one." The shove takes the enemy off their feet at "zero"; your push keeps them moving while they are a ragdoll, 1.0 s plus the landing stagger.
- **Why it works:** The rod to the face makes you the solid end. 720 N on a 130 kg enemy is 5.5 m/s2 for about 2 s: 8 m inward from the mid-deck through a rail gap and over the mouth, or along the deck off an open seam, which needs no speed at all.
- **Counter:** Hang to the deck before "zero," then the push moves nobody. The octave jump at 1.0 s of overspin is your cue to cut; the line snaps at 1.8 s and the snap is worse than the push.

## 9. The Sweep (event)

- **Where:** L5 N or S, under the Ring, within 10 s of the Ring reaching the west seam (azimuth 225 from the south side, 315 from the north).
- **Class:** Anyone. Scrappers set it fastest.
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A enemy plate. 2. Dog B the Ring's outer rail as it passes 3.5 m overhead. 3. Hang.
- **Timing:** After the Collapse only. The Ring moves at 0.9 m/s; set the line when the enemy is within 8 m of the open seam. They have 9 s before the deck ends.
- **Why it works:** The Ring is kinematic. A line attached to it is not severed by it. The rod drags them along L5 at walking pace and off the open end into the west void: 38 m onto debris, or into the Well mouth.
- **Counter:** Cut (a 9 s window; this is the gentlest kill on the map). A Hang to the deck only slips against a kinematic, so cut.

## 10. The Ring Swing (event)

- **Where:** Board the Ring at the drop ladder; ride to azimuth 225. After the Collapse.
- **Class:** Climber.
- **Inputs:** 1. Self-dog A. 2. Dog B the Ring's inner rail. 3. Hang at 15 to 20 m of rod. 4. Step off the outer edge over the void. 5. Swing under the Ring across the void and release at the far side, Z 38 to 42, onto the L5 S or N open end, or Perch a surviving ledge at Z 38 (azimuth 300). 6. Hold Haul to Reel up 4 m first if the arc is short.
- **Timing:** Between 75 and 105 s in a Scrap round while the Ring is over the void. The Ring carries the pivot with you and does not sever its own line.
- **Why it works:** The west void has no decks to hit, so a 20 m pendulum under a moving pivot is clean. Arrival at the far seam is behind anyone watching the ladder bays.
- **Counter:** Cut the line at the Ring rail when they step off (you are both on the Ring). Or be on the far seam with a Jack ready: no Kick on a player, but 600 N on a Climber whose feet have not touched yet is 6.7 m/s2 back out over the void.

## 11. Riding the Fall (event)

- **Where:** W quadrant, L5, at the 5 s siren.
- **Class:** Climber.
- **Inputs:** 1. Self-dog A. 2. Dog B the L5 W outer rail. 3. Hang, 3 m. 4. Stay with the section as the quadrant goes over. 5. At T plus 2.5 s, 10 m above the Rim W arc, cut and Perch the nearest container roof, or Dog the W floodlight mast, Hang, and Reel down.
- **Timing:** Siren at 70 s, release at 75 s. The upper levels reach the Rim at T plus 3 s.
- **Why it works:** 38 m of descent to the enemy's west alternate spawn in 3 s, arriving where nobody looks because the whole map is watching the Collapse. Land on debris: scrap heap surface, Stall threshold 24 m/s.
- **Counter:** None in the moment. Afterward the Climber is on the Rim with the slowest route back.

## 12. Lights Out

- **Where:** NE or SW broadcast tower, charges at 1.2 m on each leg. Fire from the Rim within 20 m.
- **Class:** Anyone. A Rigger follows up best.
- **Inputs:** 1. Dog A a loose steel crate or water barrel. 2. Dog B a tower-leg charge. 3. Haul. After the 2 s lean and fall: 4. Clothesline the fallen lattice at 1.2 m where it meets L1's rail.
- **Timing:** Any time. The sector goes dark at impact; lines become the only light in it (map rule 8 test case).
- **Why it works:** The charge triggers at 6 m/s. The fallen tower is a 31 m ramp to L1 that every Breaker will take, in a sector where the only thing they can see is a set line at 5 m.
- **Counter:** Crouch under the Clothesline (set above 1.0 m). Cut it. Take the lit gangway instead.
