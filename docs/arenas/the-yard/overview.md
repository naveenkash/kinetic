# THE YARD: Overview

_Arena: The Yard (Collar Four Breaking Yard). File purpose: story, feel, thesis, format, footprint, and round expectations._
_Sources: docs/bible/03-arenas.md, docs/bible/05-core-mechanics.md. Anything not in those files is marked **PROPOSED**._

## Story

The Collar Four Breaking Yard sits in the Basin, on the flat east of the Cordillera where the river slowed the wreck down. Hull sections from across the Lay come here on rail. They are cut, pressed into Weave billets, and shipped. The Lay Guild runs the Yard under an Accord license. Halvard-Teague Reclamation wants the license. The Office refuses to settle it outright and makes them fight for it one shift at a time.

The Yard does not stop for the match. The Haulers run their timetable. The Gantry picks and drops. The Shear Press cycles whether or not anyone is in it. Marshals call the match from the control shack gallery while a Guild night shift keeps cutting at the far end of the lot, offscreen, and the sparks drift across the floodlights. This is a working site, and the workers do not care who wins.

Evidence object this season (**PROPOSED**): a maintenance log in the control shack, per 01-world-and-lore's rule that every piece of evidence is a physical object recoverable from a map.

## Location in the Lay

Basin floor, Collar Four debris field, 40 km east of the Guild's training yard that hosts the Apprenticeship. Night shift. Overcast. The Basin's sodium glow sits on the underside of the cloud deck to the south.

## Who contests the claim

Guild (license holder) against HTR (challenger). Hangtown crews and Fray crews turn up as hired hands on either side. The Ascendancy sends Climbers because the Rails are the best perch in the league and they want the footage.

## What the player should feel

Timetable pressure. The Yard is the only arena where the most lethal thing on the map is on a schedule you can read off a wall. New players die to trains. Good players cross between trains. Masters put other people in front of them.

Secondary feel: canyon knife-fighting in the Stacks, where sightlines break every 10 m and a Scrapper with a scrap heap behind them is a real threat. Third: exposure on the Rails, 36 m up, where the only cover is a gantry that moves.

## Thesis

**You do not move the Yard. The Yard moves you.**

## Format

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Player count | 3v3 (Scrap, Claim, Haul). 3v3v3 supported in Scrap with the third crew dropping at the center Alternate spawn (**PROPOSED**). Free Scrap 8 solo supported. |
| Target modes | All three. Claim is the Yard's primary mode. The Yard is the Apprenticeship's first Claim Match map (docs/bible/04-progression.md). |
| Dominant class | Rigger (trench bridges, Clotheslines across the grate crossings, set lines across lanes). Breaker second (the Caboose, the Plow, the Shack Drop). |
| Weakest class | Climber in the Stacks; strongest on the Rails. |

## Round-length expectations

| Mode | Rule (05 section 9.1) | Yard expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Scrap | 2:30, best of 5 | Rounds average 95 s. Shift Change at 1:15 remaining ends most stalemates; a round that reaches Shift Change ends inside the next 30 s about 70% of the time (**PROPOSED** target). |
| Claim | 8:00, first to 100 | Marker Flat changes hands 4 to 6 times. The Shift Change drop lands 15 m from the marker and usually decides the last 60 s. |
| Haul | 2 x 4:00 | The spool rides a roller cradle (friction 0.02), so about 400 N starts it. An uncontested crew with three lines to static anchors ahead covers the 75 m in 60 to 90 s including retethers. Most pulls take 2 to 3 minutes under contest, and most are lost when the puller crew crosses a grate. |

Elimination mix target (05 section 12, adjusted for this map, **PROPOSED**): 35% object impact, 30% kinematic (Hauler and Gantry), 15% ring-out (the press), 15% player-into-world, 5% blast. If the press exceeds 25% of eliminations, widen the pit lip rail.
