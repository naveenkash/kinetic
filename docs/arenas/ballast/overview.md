# BALLAST: Overview

_Arena: Ballast. File purpose: the one-page brief for the whole arena. Sources: docs/bible/03-arenas.md, docs/bible/05-core-mechanics.md. Anything not in those two files is marked **PROPOSED**._

## Story

The freight climber _Tenacity_ was 410 m long and 200 km up when the Strand parted. It came down nose-first into the Rucu Gorge, in the Cordillera foothills below Collar Five, and stayed mostly whole. Sixty-one years later it is still there, standing at roughly seventy degrees, held in the gorge by its own crushed cargo and nothing else. Halvard-Teague Reclamation holds the demolition claim. The Lay Guild contests it on safety grounds. The matches decide who gets to kill the ship.

HTR has not waited for the verdict. Their cutters have already opened the drive head, lifted out a tier of containers, and started the cut that will shear the port side off the cargo bays. The hull has started to move. Riggers call the movement the Breathing. The cut that finishes during every match is called the Shear.

## Location in the Lay

Rucu Gorge, 40 km east of the Collar Five debris field, 1,900 m altitude. The gorge is 120 m deep at the wreck. The climber's nose is buried in the gorge floor; its tail leans against the eastern wall 380 m up. The Office's gallery hangs from the western wall opposite the opened Crown. LayCast towers stand on both rims.

## Who contests the claim

HTR (Breaker crews, usually fronted by Rook Adeyemi) against the Guild (Rigger crews, Teodora Quispe's apprentices). Every other faction fights here for the broadcast money. The Ascendancy likes the Spine. The Hangers hate the water.

## What the player should feel

Enclosed, tilted, and listened to. The hull is a steel room 36 m wide that leans a few degrees further every forty-five seconds, and everything in it tells you so: chains swing in unison, water walks across the Hold, stacks of two-ton containers creak against their rack dogs. The player should learn within two rounds that the groan one second before the hull reverses is the most important sound on the map. Then the Shear takes a wall away, afternoon sun floods the Bays from the side, and every fight on the mid tier is suddenly a fight beside a 100 m drop.

## Thesis

**Ballast is the map where the floor is a slow pendulum and the walls are temporary.**

## Footprint

The playable arena is the forward 72 m of the hull, from the torn nose cone to HTR's cut line (canon per CHANGELOG v0.2.2; the remaining 338 m of hull continues up the gorge wall as sealed vista). layout.md is the geometry authority.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Hull cross-section | 36 m wide (port to starboard, X) by 30 m deep (dorsal to Belly, Y) |
| Ground-plane footprint | 36 m (X) by 56 m (Y: the hull's horizontal run of 26 m plus its 30 m depth) |
| Playable height | 52 m (Hold floor at 0 m to the Crown gantry rail at 52 m) |
| Hull pitch | 70 degrees nominal, 64 to 76 degrees on the Breathing |
| Original deck plates | 20 degrees nose-down at nominal pitch, 14 to 26 degrees on the Breathing |

## The big numbers

- **The Breathing.** Hull pitch swings 70 ± 6 degrees on a 90 s cycle. Deck plates slide loose Medium props for 24 s of every 90. Container stacks drop from 500 N break-free to 250 N in that same window (**PROPOSED**), which opens the Avalanche to Climbers and Scrappers for 24 s of every 90. Groan 1.0 s before each reversal.
- **The Shear.** At 1:15 remaining in Scrap (4:00 in Claim, 2:00 in each Haul half), 5 s siren, then the port Bay wall (30 m by 36 m, Z 6 to 42) and both port galleries shed into the gorge.
- **The Well.** The torn nose cone at the Belly end of the Hold, kill volume from 10 m below the Hold floor across the full gorge. After the Shear, the open port side of the Hold Ledge, both Bay levels and the Crown deck edge as well.
- **Rated loads.** Six 40 t drive dogs in the Spine, four magnetic racks in the Bays, four gantry columns on the Crown.

## Round-length expectations

| Mode | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Scrap (2:30 rounds) | Average 85 to 95 s. First scrap typically in the Bays within 40 s. Rounds that reach the Shear (75 s in) end within 20 s of it in most playtests we expect. Ring-out share target 30%, below the 35% ceiling, because the Hold floor and Crown net catch most falls before the Shear |
| Claim (8:00) | Marker on the Mouth Plate over the Spine. Expect 4 to 6 marker swings. A crew that drops a drive dog onto the Plate resets the marker and clears the holders; expect this once per match |
| Haul (2 x 4:00) | 28 m of ratchet rail from the Mouth Plate to the Crown, then a 6 m tug of war to a cradle. A solo Breaker lifts the spool to the Spine head in about 17 s uncontested, a solo Climber in about 33 s. Expect deliveries in 60 to 120 s, and failures when the drive dogs come down |
