# Odile Lacroix "the Auctioneer"

- **Faction:** The Fray
- **Harness:** Doubler (130 kg)
- **Age:** 45
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Role on the broadcast:** Runs the Fray's book. Commentates her own matches live and takes bets mid-fight. The best two-line rigger in the game, and the only Face the Office has formally sanctioned for conduct unbecoming a broadcast.

## Who they are

Odile Lacroix has run the Fray's book for eleven years, which is longer than anyone has run it, because she is the first person to run it who also fights (**PROPOSED**). She came up as a pirate commentator on red matches at Collar Six, calling odds over the feed faster than the house could post them, until the house hired her and then until she was the house. She took up the harness at thirty-four because, she says, the odds are better from inside.

She is the best two-line rigger in the game. Not the loudest; that is Nnamdi. The best. She runs two lines in two modes with the ease of a woman holding two conversations, which she is also doing, because she commentates her own matches and takes bets from the audience as they happen. _"Going once."_ is what she says when a Dog seats. The Office has sanctioned her for it. She paid the fine and read the sanction aloud on the feed.

What she wants: a cut. Of the Weave, the footage, the bets, and of the Question, which she considers the most valuable open position on the Lay. She has bought evidence about the Severance from all four other factions: a Guild Ledger page, an HTR memo, an Ascendancy transcription, a Hanger's photograph. She keeps the book in her head and the evidence in four places, and she has not put the four pieces together, because an open question has resale value and a closed one does not. What she fears: that someone else closes it first.

Her rival is Teodora Quispe. Two record-keepers: the Guild's Ledger, which records everything and sells nothing, and the Fray's book, which records everything and sells all of it. They have met in matches six times. Odile has won four and says Teodora won the ones that mattered. Baskoro Hanif's younger sister, Rani Hanif, is a Doubler on Odile's crew; Odile uses this on air against Baskoro whenever LayCast lets her, and LayCast always lets her.

## Signature rig and playstyle

A player wearing Odile plays the Doubler as a position-taker.

- **The Double Gallows.** Two overhead anchors, two Dogs on one enemy's chest and back plates, Hang both at length, then hold Haul on both to Reel. The enemy hoists slowly on two rods from two directions and has to turn 150° to see the second. The Reel is slow and she has a long window to call the odds on the cut, which is the point: she narrates the whole metre a second.
- **The interlock, priced.** Sanctioned Spools never Kick against a player; her Overboard is a push. She says the Office's interlock is the only thing in the Accord that ever shortened her odds, and that the red-match book pays better for exactly that reason (**PROPOSED**).
- **The Kiss, overspun, released early.** She holds to 1.4 s, not 1.8. She has snapped a line on purpose eleven times in sanctioned play and says each one was priced in.
- **Hang and Haul.** One line Hangs a load over a doorway; the other Hauls the enemy through it. The Guillotine with both halves live at once.
- **The Caboose**, with a second line already on the next enemy.
- She splits tension (480 N per line, 720 N each at overspin) and plays around it: neither line is strong alone, so neither line is ever alone. Her lines are a pair or they are nothing.
- A player wearing her should be reading two things at once. Her kit rewards it.

## Narrative hooks

- **Arc:** Claim 4, the Anchor. She is buying the fourth piece. The season's evidence object is the Consortium inspection waiver in Tendai Mutasa's cache on the Stump; she has made Tendai an offer for it on air. If a player recovers it, Odile's Ledger entry is a receipt. If the Fray holds the Anchor, her Face goes on the Crown's broadcast board and her price for the whole set is posted under it.

- **The Office:** the sanction. She frames it in the Fray's pre-match room, in-world, visible in her LayCast segment background.
- **Evidence objects tied to her:** the four pieces, each a recoverable object in its home arena; a Fray odds board in the Anchor's Rim gallery with her chalk on it, prices for every Face, updated per Claim standing (environmental).
