# Tendai Mutasa "the Verger"

- **Faction:** The Ascendancy
- **Harness:** Climber (90 kg)
- **Age:** 52
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Role on the broadcast:** The Ascendancy's strategist and its elder. The oldest Climber in the league, which should be impossible. LayCast cuts to him when a match needs explaining and he declines to explain it.

## Who they are

Tendai Mutasa came to the Ascendancy at nineteen, from a Collar Five salvage family that wanted him in the Guild (**PROPOSED**). He climbed instead. He has been climbing for thirty-three years, and his body should have stopped letting him a decade ago. It has not, and he treats that as a fact to be used rather than a miracle to be discussed.

He is the Verger: the Ascendancy's keeper of its places, its routes, and its evidence. He plans the sect's matches, trains its Ascendants, and is Paloma Reyes-Okafor's mentor. On the feed he is quiet and exact. In a match he is famous for one thing: Jacking between two enemies so they leave in opposite directions off opposite edges. _"Separate. Then rise."_

What he wants: the Anchor Claim held two seasons running, which no one has done, and the Strand raised from the same root. What he fears: that the Ascendancy is wrong about the Question, and that he will have to be the one who says so. He holds the sect's evidence in a place no one else has climbed to, and he has read it. He is not sure it says what the sect believes it says.

He respects Teodora Quispe and will not say so. He considers Rook Adeyemi gravity with a salary. His rival is Mei-Lin Haldane: she reads structures the way he does, from the numbers, and her numbers point somewhere his faith does not. They have spoken once, off camera, about harmonics. Neither has told their faction.

## Signature rig and playstyle

A player wearing Tendai plays the Climber as a geometer. He is not fast. He is correct.

- **The Separation** (**PROPOSED** name for the bible's described move): self-dog, Dog B on an enemy, Jack toward an edge behind them, while a teammate or a set Hang holds the other enemy on the opposite side. The Verger ends up in the middle, and both enemies end up over opposite edges. In 05 terms this is Overboard performed twice from one pivot.
- **The Pendulum**, held past the apex. He cuts late and lands exactly where he said.
- **The Ascension**, as the one who calls it rather than the one who flies it. Tendai times the Updraft on comms for his crew.
- Perches on edges, not walls. Fires from the perch. Drops before the 1.5 s ends.
- He never Hauls another player. Against mass 1, a Haul is a gamble, and he does not gamble.

## Rig Calls

1. **Hoist** (2 s): right forefinger up, three small circles. The crane signal. He uses it to mean _come up_.
2. **Two Edges** (2.5 s): both arms out level, palms facing away, then both hands open and drop. The Separation, shown. Reads as a taunt and is not meant as one. (**PROPOSED**)
3. **The Root** (3 s): Ascendancy prayer gesture. Kneels on one knee, right palm flat on the surface, head bowed, then rises with the palm turning up. Performed on the Crown it is also a claim. (**PROPOSED**)

## Scrap Card

Ascendancy template: an altitude card. White field, the sect mark top left, a vertical scale on the right. The victim's name in silver, the height they were at when they were scrapped, and the height they ended at. Tendai's variant adds a second number in small type: the height he was at. It is always higher. Rope Mark variants add a thin line diagram of the rig. (**PROPOSED**)

## Narrative hooks

- **Arc:** Claim 4, the Anchor. The evidence object is a Consortium inspection waiver, sealed in a Stump maintenance clamp housing, at a perch on the Stump's shoulder that no one else has climbed to. Tendai cached it there in L.47 (**PROPOSED**). A player who reaches it logs it to their Ledger; Tendai's voice line on recovery is the only time he sounds tired.
- **The waiver:** it is signed by the Consortium's inspection office and waives a harmonic re-test on the Collar Four coupling, L.-1. It does not say the Strand was shot. The Ascendancy has not seen it. He has.
- **Mei-Lin:** her harmonics models and this waiver describe the same failure. A LayCast segment in Claim 4 puts them in the same frame for the first time.
- **Paloma:** he taught her the Ascension. He has never done it himself on camera. Whether he can is a question the feed asks every season.
- **Evidence objects tied to him:** the waiver (Anchor); a chalk route map on the Stump's inner scaffold in his hand, showing perches that do not exist yet (environmental, Anchor).
