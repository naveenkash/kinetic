# Paloma Reyes-Okafor "Swift"

- **Faction:** The Ascendancy
- **Harness:** Climber
- **Age:** 31
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Role on the broadcast:** Ascendant. Holds the broadcast record for a full Anchor match without touching the ground, which the Office measured because viewers demanded it. The Ascension clip is the most-replayed clip type on LayCast and most of the top ten are hers.

## Who they are

Paloma Reyes-Okafor does not touch the floor. In a match this is a method. Between matches it is a belief. She is an Ascendant of the Ascendancy, trained by Tendai Mutasa, who is her Verger, and she holds that the climb is prayer and that down is a direction and not a destination. She fights in silence. She prays aloud between rounds, on the open channel, which her sponsor Meridian Mutual has asked her three times to stop doing (**PROPOSED**). She has not answered them.

She wants every gram of Weave at the Anchor. She wants the Anchor Claim and the Crown, and she has stood on the Crown more than anyone alive. She wants the Question answered as the Ascendancy already knows it: a shot from the ground. What she fears is the floor. Not falling; landing.

She was born in L.30, thirty years after the Severance, in the Ascendancy community that grew up at the Collar Seven fall site (**PROPOSED**). Her grandmother, Inés Reyes (**PROPOSED**), lived on Collar Seven, the one above the break, and survived its fall. Paloma was raised on that story and speaks of it as inherited memory, in the first person and without metaphor: "I remember falling." The Ascendancy treats the memory as hers. She has never claimed it was anything else, and she has never claimed it was anything less.

Relationships: Tendai Mutasa is the one person she defers to. She watches Rook Adeyemi the way a bird watches a dog. She has never spoken to Mei-Lin Haldane and has been scrapped by her twice, both times by a structure. Her rival Face is Dela Santos. They are the two riggers in the league who can stay off the floor for a round, and Dela's pirate stream runs Paloma's between-round prayers with a comedy sound bed. Paloma has watched it. She said nothing, then beat Dela to the Crown by four seconds the next time they met, and said nothing again.

## Costume and harness personalization

- **Under the harness:** white sleeveless wrap top, white wrapped forearms and hands, loose white trousers tied at the ankle.
- **Over the harness:** nothing. The Climber harness is already almost nothing.
- **Personal items:** a small mirror-steel medallion on the chest cross-strap, stamped with the Ascendancy's root-and-rise mark (**PROPOSED**). Nothing else. She carries no weight she does not need.
- **Tape:** silver `#C9CDD2` on white, a single continuous spiral with no bands. Rope Mark tape at Mark tier for the Slingshot and the Pendulum, placed where the spiral begins.
- **Tallies:** four sockets filled, Office white on mirror, which is hard to read and the prep crew has complained about. Strokes in fives.
- **Sponsor:** Meridian Mutual patch, 5 cm, on the left hip strap. The patch is the only thing on her that is not white, silver, or mirror. She has asked for it to be smaller.
- **Dogs:** Standard, needle-thin visible mesh on the standard 8 cm collision sphere, mirror finish. Seat sound is a ping.
- **Cutter:** Standard. White-wrapped handle, plain blade. Sheathed on the inner left forearm per the Climber spec, blade toward the elbow. Ascendancy serial engraved in the wrap-mark script (**PROPOSED**).
- **Altitude numerals:** 300 on the chest plate, hand-painted, white. The highest number in the league.
- **Silhouette:** locked Climber. The medallion and the hip patch are under 2 cm.

## Signature rig and playstyle

The Slingshot, the Pendulum, the Ascension. A player wearing Paloma is expected to never touch the floor: chain Slingshots wall to wall, Pendulum through the mid tier, perch for 1.5 s to fire, and go again. On the Anchor, ride the Updraft at the top of its cycle from a Stump Slingshot to the Crown. She does not set traps. She wins by never being where traps are and by Hauling lighter things into heavier things on the way past. Against a Breaker she stays out of 20 m and makes them waste Dogs. The moment a Paloma player stands still, they are a projectile.

## Voice

Silent in a match. This is the character and the voice pack honors it: most triggers produce breath, a single word, or nothing. Her speaking voice, heard between rounds and in the lobby, is a warm mezzo, low and even, Peruvian Spanish-inflected English direction, unhurried, with the cadence of recited text. The bible line is used under "round loss."

## Drop entry

Sliding a line, class default. 4.5 s.

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0.0 s | A high anchor above the spawn, silhouetted against the sky. A line already runs from it down toward the floor, dim and slack. |
| 0.6 s | Her wrapped hand closes on the line, high in frame. |
| 1.2 s | Wide shot. She slides, body straight, feet together, one hand on the line, the other extended. The white wraps catch the light. |
| 2.8 s | At 2 m above the floor she lets go, turns a half rotation in the air, and lands on the balls of her feet on a rail, not the floor. |
| 3.6 s | Close. She touches the medallion with two fingers, then points them straight up. |
| 4.2 s | Her eyes close for one breath. They open on the structure. |
| 4.5 s | Cut to game. She has not touched the floor. |

## Scrap Card

Ascendancy template: an altitude card. White stock, silver rule, a vertical scale down the left edge marked in meters. The victim's name sits at the height they were scrapped from, with a thin horizontal line drawn across to the scale. Above it, a small mark at Paloma's height at the moment of the scrap, which is almost always higher. The card reads, in the Ascendancy's wrap-mark script and plain text: "[name]. [height] m. Below."

## Narrative hooks

- **Claim 4, the Anchor.** Her arc. The Ascension, the Crown, and the evidence object: a Consortium inspection waiver sealed in a Stump maintenance clamp housing, Tendai's cache. Only a Climber can reach it without a Breaker pulling the clamp. If the Ascendancy wins the Claim, her Face goes on the Crown's board and she stands under it for the full between-season bulletin without speaking (**PROPOSED**).
- **Collar Seven.** The fall site where her grandmother survived and where Paloma was raised is a backlog arena concept (the Climb, Ascendancy home ground, no floor). Her Ledger entries about it are written in the first person present tense, in her grandmother's voice and hers, and the Ledger does not mark where one ends (**PROPOSED**).
- **The memory.** Inherited memory is the canon. Whether Inés Reyes saw anything at 04:12 that bears on the Question is a writers' room decision, and if she did, Paloma already knows it. If the Question's answer is a shot from the ground, her grandmother was right. If not, Paloma was still on the Crown.
- **Meridian Mutual.** A Contract reward for Ascendancy allegiance is her harness with the sponsor patch removed. The Office bulletin notes the breach and the fine (**PROPOSED**).
