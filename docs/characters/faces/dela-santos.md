# Dela Santos "Kite"

- **Faction:** Hangtown Crews (Hangers)
- **Harness:** Scrapper (110 kg)
- **Age:** 17
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Role on the broadcast:** Layborn prodigy. The fastest Cutter anyone has measured. Streams her own matches on a pirate channel with better commentary than LayCast's, and LayCast knows it.

## Who they are

Dela Santos was born in the Low Hang, under the drip nets, in L.44. She has never been more than forty kilometers from the ravine. She learned the Spool from pirate streams of Fray red matches and Guild Claim Matches at eleven, on a Spool her mother's crew had written off, and refused Teodora Quispe's apprenticeship at fourteen because she already knew how (**PROPOSED**). She was wrong about that and right about everything else.

She cuts before the line finishes singing. The Office measured her once at 0.11 s from Dog seat to blade contact on a chest plate, which is faster than the Scrapper's Hot Cutter warm-up and means she started moving before the Dog landed (**PROPOSED** figure). _"Nope."_ Said while cutting. It is the most-clipped single word on the feed.

What she wants: to be watched. Not for the money. She wants the pirate channel's numbers to beat LayCast's for one match. What she fears: being bought. HTR has offered her a contract four times. The fourth offer is still open and she has not said no to it, which is not like her, and Jesús has noticed.

She is reckless in a way that reads as joy, and it is joy. Her rival is Paloma Reyes-Okafor: two riggers who treat the floor as optional, one who prays about it and one who makes fun of the praying on stream. Dela has clipped Paloma's between-round prayers with her own commentary over them. Paloma has never responded. That bothers Dela more than she says.

## Signature rig and playstyle

A player wearing Dela plays the Scrapper as a reflex.

- **The cut.** Her defining play is not a rig. Any Dog that seats on her is a 0.15 s problem. Players wearing Dela should feel the panic window as a routine.
- **Overboard**, from a slide. Self-dog, Dog B on the enemy, Jack with the edge behind them, and she is lighter than everyone, so she brings a Hang to the floor behind her first.
- **The Slingshot**, chained, badly, with enthusiasm.
- **Cut-Loose** in Hangtown: she cuts swing bridges and slack cables faster than anyone because she is already holding the knife.
- **Recall** as a feint. She fires Dog A at an enemy's chest so they turn to cut it, recalls it instantly, and fires the real one into their back plate.
- She cannot move anything heavy and does not try. A Breaker is a wall she goes around.

## Rig Calls

1. **Use Whip Line** (1.5 s): taps the left elbow with the right hand, then points. The crane signal for the fast auxiliary hoist. She uses it to mean _I'm doing it, keep up_.
2. **Chat** (2 s): holds the Spool forearm up like a phone, screen out, and mouths at it. The pirate channel's sign-on. (**PROPOSED**)
3. **Nope** (1.5 s): draws the Cutter, flicks it once across nothing, sheaths it. The shortest Rig Call in the game. (**PROPOSED**)

## Narrative hooks

- **Arc:** Claim 3, Hangtown. The contract. HTR's fourth offer was arranged on Mei-Lin Haldane's recommendation; Mei-Lin watched Dela's stream, not LayCast. If HTR wins the Claim and cuts the district, the offer becomes the only thing keeping Dela's family under a roof, and the season's final LayCast segment is her reading it on stream, out loud, and not saying yes or no.
- **Paloma:** Dela mocks the prayers. In Claim 4, at the Anchor, the Updraft puts the two of them on the Crown together for the first time. Paloma speaks to her. The line is withheld from this document on purpose; it is a writers' room beat.
- **Teodora:** the refused apprenticeship. Teodora has a Ledger page for Dela with no entry on it except a date and the words _knows how_.
- **Jesús:** he is the closest thing to a crew lead she listens to. He has not asked her about the contract. She wishes he would.
- **The Office:** two letters about the pirate channel, one about the earbuds. The Office has not shut her down because her numbers are good for the sport, and the Office's position on everything is undetermined.
- **Evidence objects tied to her:** none directly. Her stream archive is an in-world source: Ledger entries in Claim 3 are written as her stream clips, timestamped.
