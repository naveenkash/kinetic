# Rook Adeyemi "Wreckingball"

- **Faction:** Halvard-Teague Reclamation
- **Harness:** Breaker
- **Age:** 41
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Role on the broadcast:** HTR's star and the heaviest harness in sanctioned play. The villain the feed cuts to when it wants a quote. He gives one every time.

## Who they are

Rook Adeyemi was Guild. He came through Teodora Quispe's yard, made Lead by thirty, and was the Guild's best Heavy for a decade. In L.52 he led an unsanctioned match at Collar Six. A Guild Hand named Iker Ballesteros died on a dark line. Teodora was that Hand's Wright. Rook left the Guild the following week and signed with HTR the week after that. Nobody on either side talks about it on a broadcast, which is why the broadcast keeps asking (**PROPOSED** detail: he has never said whether the dark line was his).

He believes, and says, that the Guild is a trade union for a trade that should have ended thirty years ago. He believes the Lay is inventory. He believes the kindest thing anyone can do for the people living in the wreck is take the wreck away from them. He says it without heat, which is worse. What he wants is simple: every Collar down and shipped before he is too old to pull. What he fears is height. A Breaker in the air is cargo, and he knows precisely how long his Cutter takes.

His relationship to the Question is HTR's: micrometeoroid, nobody's fault, close the file. He does not believe it. He has decided it does not matter, and he is angrier about Teodora knowing something than about not knowing it himself.

Relationships: Mei-Lin Haldane is on his crew. She calls the failure, he pulls. He trusts her numbers more than he has ever trusted a person. He has a grudging regard for Jesús Vilca, who hates him for the right reasons. His rival Face is Teodora Quispe. She does not say his name. He says hers constantly.

## Costume and harness personalization

- **Under the harness:** HTR orange compression layer, long sleeves, high collar. Grey work trousers.
- **Over the harness:** nothing. HTR does not allow it.
- **Personal items:** one. His Cutter is the Cutter he received from the Guild at Hand grade. Nobody lends a Cutter and nobody surrenders one; HTR could not make him. The handle is re-wrapped in HTR grey but the Guild serial is still at the guard (**PROPOSED**: 1188).
- **Tape:** HTR grey and orange `#FF5A1F`, machine-wrapped, and then one hand-wrapped black band at the line exit, 2 cm. The Office allows it. Teodora notices it. Rope Mark tape at Mark tier for the Avalanche, the Anchor Drop, and the Caboose.
- **Tallies:** none visible. HTR replaces his plates every match, so the prep crew repaints his career count onto the fresh chest plate before each Drop, in Office white, and it is the largest tally block on any Face (**PROPOSED**).
- **Sponsor:** HTR logo field on the back plate; Kessl Harness Leasing tag riveted lower-left chest, sequential serial.
- **Dogs:** HTR machined Standard, matte grey, with a Mag option on steel maps. Oversized by cosmetic allowance; seat sound is a heavy clank with a low tail.
- **Cutter:** Standard, handle described above. Cross-draw from the right hip per the Breaker spec. His draw is the slowest in the game and he does not hurry it.
- **Silhouette:** locked Breaker. The black tape band is the only thing on him that is not HTR's.

## Signature rig and playstyle

The Avalanche, the Anchor Drop, the Caboose with himself as the train. A player wearing Rook is expected to ignore enemies and Haul structure: the bottom container, the rated column, the counterweight. Brace early and often. Let Climbers waste Dogs on a braced 220 kg. Use the 1,000 N to move what nobody else can move (the rated break-free is exactly 1,000 N) and let gravity resolve the room. He never Jacks off a wall. He never leaves the ground on purpose.

## Scrap Card

HTR template: a work order. Grey card stock, orange header bar, a barcode down the right edge that scans to the victim's Ledger number. Line items: "ITEM: [name]. STATUS: DEMOLISHED. METHOD: [rig]. SCHEDULED: [match clock]." A rubber stamp slams down last, slightly off-square: COMPLETE. His is the only HTR card with a stamp; Mei-Lin's has a checkbox.
