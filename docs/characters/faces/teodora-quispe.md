# Teodora Quispe "Mother Line"

- **Faction:** Lay Guild
- **Harness:** Rigger
- **Age:** 72
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Role on the broadcast:** Wright of the Lay Guild. The Guild's senior Face. Runs the apprenticeship, so she is the first voice every player hears. LayCast uses her for the pre-match "state of the trade" segment because she will not perform for them.

## Who they are

Teodora Quispe is the Guild. Not its founder, but the person the Guild means when it says "the trade." She was born in L.-11 in the Collar Four maintenance quarters, was fourteen when the Guild wrote the First Rule in L.3, and was fifteen the next year, pulling ribbon out of the Cordillera under rifle fire. She did not write the First Rule. She has enforced it for fifty-seven years, which is why she does not raise her voice about it. She is a Wright, keeps her own section of the Ledger by hand, and has trained more Greenhands than anyone alive, including Baskoro Hanif (now her Lead), Rook Adeyemi (who left), and Jesús Vilca (who did his apprenticeship and went home to Hangtown). She did not train Mei-Lin Haldane and watches her closely because of it. Dela Santos refused the apprenticeship outright, which Teodora respects more than she says.

She wants Weave spent on the Collars people live in before anyone spends it on the sky. She wants the Question answered honestly, because the riggers reported the harmonics in L.-1 and were ignored. She fears one specific thing: another Collar Six. In L.52 a Guild Hand named Iker Ballesteros died on a dark line in an unsanctioned match at Collar Six. Rook Adeyemi was his Lead. Teodora was his Wright. Rook left the Guild the week after. She has not said his name on a broadcast since, and the broadcast has noticed (**PROPOSED**).

Her relationship to the Question is direct: she knows what the harmonics logs said and she knows who signed off on them. She has not said so because she has no object to point at. The Yard's control shack has the object.

## Costume and harness personalization

- **Under the harness:** faded ochre canvas work shirt, sleeves rolled twice, collar worn through. Dark wool trousers. A chalk bag on the belt.
- **Over the harness:** nothing. The harness is the uniform.
- **Personal items:** a pocket Ledger in a canvas sleeve on the chest cross-strap, pencil stub tied to it. A piece of yellow lumber chalk. She marks anchors with it between rounds, which does nothing mechanically and tells her crew everything (**PROPOSED**).
- **Tape:** ochre `#C8862B` with black `#141211` bands of exactly equal width, 2 cm each. Never re-taped mid-match. Rope Mark bands for the Gallows, Guillotine, and Clothesline at Mark tier sit against the Spool.
- **Tallies:** the most on any Face. All eight tally sockets filled, strokes in groups of five, Office white, oldest ones overpainted three times.

## Signature rig and playstyle

The Gallows. A player wearing Teodora is expected to play the Rigger as written: set the room, hold the door, let the structure do the work. Two set lines at 12 s, placed before contact. Hang an overhead anchor to an approaching enemy's plate, then hold Haul to Reel: the rod shortens at 1 m/s and they hoist, slowly, bearing up to her 12 kN Hang load, which means she hoists Breakers. The Gallows is slow by design and she plays it that way: the victim's window is long, and she spends it setting the next line under where they will land if they cut. She also plays the Guillotine and the Clothesline, and her Rope Marks for all three are at Mark tier. She never sprints into a fight. She braces early and lets Breakers waste their tension on her.

## Voice

Low alto, unhurried, rough with age at the edges, with the breath control of someone who has shouted across yards for fifty years and chooses not to. Andean Spanish-inflected English direction. Short declaratives, full stops, a pause before the important word. She never raises her voice. The bible line is used under "scrapped someone."

## Narrative hooks

- **Claim 1, the Yard.** The Collar Four harmonics maintenance log, L.-1, sits in the Yard's control shack. Teodora was ten that year, living in the Collar Four maintenance quarters. The reporting Hand on the log is her mother, Rosa Quispe, a Strand maintenance Hand who filed it and was overruled (**PROPOSED**). The sign-off signatures below her mother's are the names Teodora has never said. She heard them argued over a kitchen table. This is the object that lets her speak.
- **Claim 4, the Anchor.** She wants the Anchor Claim not to raise the Strand but to stop anyone else doing it before the Collars are safe. If the Guild wins it, her Face goes on the Crown's broadcast board and she asks for it to be taken down (**PROPOSED** Office bulletin).
- **Collar Six.** Iker Ballesteros's Ledger line is the first evidence object a Guild-allegiance player unlocks (**PROPOSED** Ledger entry, three sentences).
- **The apprenticeship.** Her voice pack begins calling the player by Grade once they reach Hand. At Wright she calls them by name once.
