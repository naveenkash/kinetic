# Jesús Vilca "Fuse"

- **Faction:** Hangtown Crews (Hangers)
- **Harness:** Scrapper (110 kg)
- **Age:** 36
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Role on the broadcast:** Hangtown's crew lead. The poorest faction's best demolitions man. LayCast's commentators call him Fuse. Everyone in Vilca's Hang calls him Chucho.

## Who they are

Jesús Vilca was born in a house that hangs. His grandmother, Amparo Vilca, strung the first cable across the Collar Three ravine in L.4 (**PROPOSED** date), and the district that grew on it carries her name. He grew up fixing it. At nineteen he took the Guild apprenticeship under Teodora Quispe, got his Cutter and his serial, and went home the same month, because HTR had posted the first condemnation notice on the market ring and nobody in the Hang could read structural drawings except him (**PROPOSED**).

He times barrels. That is the short version of his reputation and it is accurate: nobody in the league reads a volatile's arc and a target's path and puts them in the same place at the same second like Chucho. _"That was already going to explode. I just moved up the schedule."_

What he wants: Hangtown standing at the end of every Claim. What he fears: the season he loses it, and having to tell three thousand people which quarter is gone. He is wry and tired on the feed, and he is furious in a way he only shows when HTR is in the match. Rook Adeyemi fronts HTR's Hangtown claim on camera, which makes Rook his rival, and Jesús does not hide it.

He still calls Teodora Wright. He treats Dela Santos like the kid sister who refused to go to school and turned out faster than anyone who did. He and Nnamdi Ferreira have a running argument, conducted entirely through the Kiss, about whether timing or tension is the honest way to make two barrels meet.

## Signature rig and playstyle

A player wearing Jesús plays the Scrapper as a demolitions man with a clock in his head.

- **The Kiss**, timed. Dog A on a propane cylinder, Dog B on a second, Haul, with the meeting point placed on an enemy's path one second from now. Racked cylinders need 200 N break-free, which his 400 N clears; a stacked crate's 500 N he cannot, and he does not try.
- **The Scatter**, when the enemy is spread. He fires it at 30 m/s separation into two groups and walks away before it lands.
- **Anything with a cart in it.** A cart carrying a drum into a doorway is his Clothesline.
- **Overboard**, constantly, because Hangtown has no floor.
- Instant recall means a line he does not like is gone before the enemy reads it. Play him as someone who fires three lines to land one.
- He loses every straight pull. Against a Breaker he never takes one. He makes the Breaker stand next to something.

## Rig Calls

1. **Dog Everything** (2 s): both hands clasped in front of the body, elbows out. The crane signal for _hold everything where it is_. He uses it to tell a crew to stop and listen to the structure.
2. **Schedule** (2.5 s): taps the wrist where a watch is not, holds up three fingers, two, one, then opens the hand flat. A countdown with nothing at the end of it. (**PROPOSED**)
3. **Notice** (3 s): mimes pulling a paper off a wall, reads it, crumples it, drops it. The rude one. Hangers use it at HTR. (**PROPOSED**)

## Scrap Card

Hangers template: a condemnation notice. Yellow paper field, Office header, the victim's name typed into the blank where the address goes, a district stencil sprayed over the whole thing. Jesús's variant: the stencil is Vilca's Hang (cable and house), the notice has a chalk tally mark added in the corner, and the stamp at the bottom reads SCHEDULED instead of CONDEMNED. Rope Mark variants add a small barrel icon. (**PROPOSED**)

## Narrative hooks

- **Arc:** Claim 3, Hangtown. If the Hangers hold it, the district stands. If HTR wins, next season a quarter of the map is gone, and the cables hang empty, and Jesús's pre-match segment is recorded standing in front of them.
- **The evidence object:** a Collar Three interior camera drive, salvaged by Amparo Vilca in L.4, hidden in a market-ring house. It recorded the sky at 04:12. Jesús does not know it is there. His grandmother did.
- **Teodora:** she signed his Cutter serial into the Ledger and wrote _went home_ next to it. He has seen the entry. He does not know what she meant by it.
- **Rook:** HTR's Hangtown claim is read on camera by Rook Adeyemi every season. Jesús has asked LayCast, on air, whether Rook has ever been inside one of the houses. The clip is popular.
- **Nnamdi:** a Kiss landed by either of them within sight of the other gets a specific bark. The feud is friendly and neither admits it.
- **Dela:** HTR's fourth contract offer to Dela is still open. If she takes it, the Hang loses its best Cutter and Jesús loses the argument he has been making about staying.
- **Evidence objects tied to him:** Amparo's camera drive (Hangtown); the original cable's anchor tag, stamped 01, on the Collar Three rim (environmental, Hangtown); the condemnation notices on his own door, which change text per Claim result.
