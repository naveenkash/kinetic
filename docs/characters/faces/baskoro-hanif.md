# Baskoro Hanif "Hitch"

- **Faction:** Lay Guild
- **Harness:** Rigger
- **Age:** 29
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Role on the broadcast:** Guild Lead. The Guild's active-roster star and its best hope of holding Hangtown. The feed cuts to his hands between rounds because viewers want to watch him re-tape.

## Who they are

Baskoro Hanif came through Teodora Quispe's apprenticeship at sixteen, was a Hand at eighteen, and a Lead at twenty-four, which the Ledger lists as the youngest Guild Lead since the Ribbon Wars (**PROPOSED**). He is precise to the point of ritual. He sets five lines before an enemy sees one and counts them under his breath on the open channel. Viewers turned the count into a drinking game. He knows, and has not stopped, because stopping would be a change and he does not change things that work.

He wants the Guild to hold Hangtown, because three thousand people live there and because Teodora asked him to. He wants his sister back. Rani Hanif, 25, fights as a Doubler on Odile Lacroix's Fray crew (**PROPOSED**). She left the Guild apprenticeship at Hand grade for the Fray's money and the Fray's audience. They speak. They do not speak about it. When the Guild draws the Fray, LayCast puts a split-screen on the siblings and Baskoro's count gets quieter.

He fears exactly what a methodical person fears: the thing he did not set a line for. His relationship to the Question is secondhand and loyal. Teodora knows something. He has never asked. He assumes she will tell him when there is a thing to do about it.

Relationships: Teodora is his Wright and the closest thing he has to a parent on the Lay. Jesús Vilca was two years ahead of him in the yard and he still calls him "Lead" out of habit. He finds Rook Adeyemi's broadcast contempt for the Guild easier to take than Rani's silence. His rival Face is Odile Lacroix, who runs the book his sister fights under and who has, twice, taken a live bet on which Hanif would scrap the other.

## Costume and harness personalization

- **Under the harness:** pressed canvas shirt in Guild ochre, sleeves buttoned at the wrist, collar closed. Dark trousers with a sharp crease. He irons.
- **Over the harness:** nothing.
- **Personal items:** a roll of fresh ochre tape and a roll of black on the belt, left side, always two. A small steel comb in the chest pocket (**PROPOSED**).
- **Tape:** ochre `#C8862B` and black `#141211` in counted groups: five ochre bands, one black, repeated. He re-tapes the Spool between every round; the bands are always fresh and always aligned. Rope Mark tape at Mark tier for the Clothesline and the Guillotine, Band tier for the Gallows.
- **Tallies:** six of eight sockets filled, strokes in fives, Office white, neat.
- **Sponsor:** none. Lease plate stamped CLEAR last Claim; the stamp is still bright.
- **Dogs:** Standard, Guild pattern, brass, with a painted numeral on each: 1 and 2, in black. He counts them too.
- **Cutter:** Standard. Handle wrapped in black cord in a tight continuous whip, no gaps. Guild serial 2217 (**PROPOSED**). Left thigh sheath per the Rigger spec.
- **Silhouette:** locked Rigger. The only visible personalization at 50 m is the band count on the tape and the two rolls on the belt.

## Signature rig and playstyle

The Clothesline and the Guillotine, in that order. A player wearing Baskoro is expected to play ahead of the fight: both set lines placed at 12 s, one across the doorway at 1.2 m, one holding a load overhead. Then the live line for the Gallows when someone crouches under the first. He plays the count: a Hanif player should be re-setting the oldest line as it expires, so there are always two. He brakes at doors and lets enemies arrive. Against a Breaker he gives ground and sets again behind it. He does not Slingshot.

## Rig Calls

| Name | Gesture | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Five** | Right hand held up, fingers counted off one at a time, thumb last. Then a flat hand: done. | 2.5 s |
| **Slack** | Left arm out, hand making a slow slackening wave downward, the rigging signal to pay out line. He uses it as "relax, I have this." | 2.0 s |
| **Mind the Door** | Two fingers point at his own eyes, then at the nearest doorway, then a flat hand at chest height. Shared with Teodora's call; his is faster. | 1.5 s |

## Scrap Card

Guild template: a Ledger page. His is printed-neat in pencil capitals with ruled columns. The victim's name goes in the first column. The second column is a numeral: which of his lines did it, "LINE 3." The third is the rig. A fourth column, narrow, holds a tick. The tick is drawn last.

## Narrative hooks

- **Claim 3, Hangtown.** His arc. The Guild and the Hangers contest HTR's claim; Baskoro is the Guild's Lead on it. The evidence object is a Collar Three interior camera drive salvaged by Jesús Vilca's grandmother, hidden in a market-ring house. Baskoro finds it because he set a Guillotine from the house's beam and the beam gave (**PROPOSED** Ledger entry). He hands it to Teodora unopened.
- **Rani.** A Fray-allegiance player who scraps Baskoro while wearing a Doubler unlocks a Ledger line in Rani's voice (**PROPOSED**, three sentences). A Guild player who scraps Rani's crew unlocks his.
- **The count.** His Contract line for the Guild: "Hang a line that someone else uses. Twenty times." His voice pack counts the player's set lines aloud at Rigger grade and above.
