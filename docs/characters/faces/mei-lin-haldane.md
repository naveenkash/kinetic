# Mei-Lin Haldane "the Surveyor"

- **Faction:** Halvard-Teague Reclamation
- **Harness:** Breaker
- **Age:** 24
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Role on the broadcast:** HTR's structural caller. The most-clipped Face on the feed because she announces a collapse before it happens and is almost never wrong. LayCast overlays her calls with a countdown graphic. She did not ask for it.

## Who they are

Mei-Lin Haldane is Academy-trained. HTR's Reclamation Academy took her at seventeen on a structural engineering track and put her in a harness at twenty-one because her failure models were better in the field than on paper. She has never worked a real demolition shift. She has never needed to. She reads a structure like a spreadsheet: loads, members, margins, time. She says the number on comms and her crew stands clear.

She wants to be right. That is the whole of it, and it is why she is in trouble. Her models of the Strand's harmonics, built on Academy time with Academy data, match the Guild's account of L.-1. The Strand was singing wrong and the Consortium knew. She has not told HTR. She has not told anyone. She fears being wrong less than she fears the number being right and having to decide what to do about it.

Her relationship to the Question is the most dangerous on the roster because it is a spreadsheet, not a belief. Relationships: Rook Adeyemi is her Lead. She calls, he pulls; she thinks he is a blunt instrument with a conscience he will not use. She recommended HTR make Dela Santos an offer after watching four of Dela's streams, on the grounds that the fastest Cutter in the league should not belong to a neighborhood (**PROPOSED**). She has never met Teodora Quispe and avoids it. Her rival Face is Tendai Mutasa: two people who read structures, one with numbers and one with faith, and both are usually right.

## Signature rig and playstyle

The Avalanche and the Anchor Drop, timed. A player wearing Mei-Lin is expected to play the Breaker's rated-load kit with a clock: Haul the column at the moment the enemy crew is under the consequence, not before. She holds the 1.0 s minimum groan tell against the enemy's position and pulls so the consequence lands at the end of their panic window. Brace to hold a line against a Haul until the structure resolves it. She never chases. She does not need to.

## Scrap Card

HTR template: a work order. Hers is the inventory-tag variant: grey stock, orange header, barcode on the right. Line items: "ITEM: [name]. FAILURE MODE: [rig]. PREDICTED: [her call time]. ACTUAL: [match clock]." The two times are almost always within a second. A checkbox is ticked last with a white marker, not a stamp.

## Narrative hooks

- **Claim 1, the Yard.** The Collar Four harmonics maintenance log, L.-1, in the control shack. An HTR-allegiance player who recovers it unlocks a Ledger entry in her voice that is four numbers and the sentence "These match mine." (**PROPOSED**) It is the first time the audience learns she has a model.
- **Claim 2, Ballast.** The Tenacity flight recorder. Its 04:12 telemetry includes a harmonic trace. Her Ledger entry on it is longer than her usual, and ends unfinished (**PROPOSED**).
- **The decision.** By the end of a year of Claims she has to tell someone. The writers' room should decide who: HTR, Teodora, or the feed. The Face docs assume nothing.
- **Dela.** If Dela Santos ever signs, Mei-Lin is the reason. If she never does, Mei-Lin's fourth offer is still open and it is in her handwriting.
