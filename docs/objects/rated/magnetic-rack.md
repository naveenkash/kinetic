# Magnetic Rack

One-line: rated load, structural, 5,500 kg frame (carrying up to three 2,000 kg containers), 6.4 m long x 2.6 m deep x 8.2 m tall, Ballast Bays: fourteen racks bolted to the bay decks and walls, four of them live rated loads per round (two port, two starboard; the port pair is taken by the Shear).

## Role in play

A container stack is a Rigger's Avalanche. The rack holding it is a Breaker's. Commit a live rack and the whole frame tears off the deck and goes over with its stack as one unit, 11,500 kg, onto whatever the Bay is doing. The rack's top beam also carries the ends of a B-Low catwalk, so the catwalk goes with it. It turns a bay from a canyon into a slope.

| Harness | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Climber | Rack tops are 8 m perches. A rack with a line on it from a Breaker is a perch to leave. |
| Scrapper | Static terrain until it falls; then eleven tons of spilled Medium contents. |
| Rigger | Hang anchor. A Rigger Guillotines a barrel from the rack's top beam. |
| Doubler | Overspin commits it; the Doubler is staggered 8 m from eleven tons of falling rack. Do it from the catwalk above. |
| Breaker | Owner. Haul the rack's uphill post from the far side of the bay, braced, on the lean toward the target. |

Read by tier: from the Crown, dark frames with pale container tops. From the Bays, taped posts at the canyon mouths. From the Hold, the rack bottoms through the deck grating.

## Variants

1. **Live, loaded.** Three containers. 11,500 kg total. The standard rated play.
2. **Live, light.** One cut container (1,400 kg). 6,900 kg. Falls faster, less debris. Used on the Claim-marker bay so the marker is not buried every round.
3. **Dead.** Bolts intact, untaped. Static. Thirteen of fourteen in a stabilized season.
4. **Sheared.** Port racks after the Shear: gone with the wall; base plates remain as 0.3 m trip stubs with the tape torn.

## Physics

- Tier: Rated. Break-free 1,000 N: a Breaker alone, a Doubler at overspin, or any lines summing to 1,000 N across the fall axis (a crew; 05 section 6.2). The 1,000 N is a 0.6 s commit trigger; after it the authored timeline carries the rack regardless of friction or line. The rack is bolted; its live-variant bolts are cut, so it is held by its own weight on the deck and two remaining bolts that shear at the commit.
- Commit: 1,000 N sustained 0.6 s (README). The pull direction matters: the consequence fires only if the line's horizontal component points across the bay (within 45 degrees of the rack's authored fall axis). A pull along the rack's length sings and shudders and never commits. The Guild chalk arrow shows the axis.
- Fall: the rack tips about its downhill base plates. 8.2 m tall, center of mass at 4.5 m with containers: tip time from release to deck impact about 1.9 s (authored). The top beam travels through a 8.2 m arc and arrives at the deck at roughly 9 m/s.
- Impacts: top beam on a player, 5,500 kg at 9 m/s, scrapped. Containers leave the rack at 0.6 m of tip and arrive separately 0.3 s later, then rest on the bare deck as fixed scenery (05 section 6.1a). The B-Low catwalk whose end sits on the top beam fractures (2,500 N s) at release.
- On the lean: if the Breathing's pitch is toward the fall axis at commit, the fall takes 1.6 s and the containers slide a further 2 m after landing. Against the lean: 2.3 s, containers stop where they land. The lean is 6 degrees; the difference is readable and is the skill.
- Crush: a player pinned between a tipping rack and the opposite rack: over 8 kN immediately, scrapped.

## Authored consequence

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0.0 s | Commit. Lurch: rack shifts 60 mm on its base plates, two bolt heads pop. |
| 0.0 to 1.0 s | Groan, 1.0 s lead. Posts flex, magnet discs crack and fall as Light props (0.8 kg, 10 s), the `NO HOLD` placards swing. Marshal: "Rack going in bay two." |
| 1.0 s | Release. Downhill base plates become hinges. Catwalk end fractures with its own tell. |
| 1.0 to 2.9 s | Tip. Containers detach at 0.6 m, doors blow open on the loaded variant: 4 fuel drums or 6 propane per container released as Medium props along the fall path (budgeted: 12 props max). |
| 2.9 s | Impact. Deck decal 8 m x 3 m, dust volume 6 m for 3 s (visual only; no force). Any container landing door-down spills. |
| After | The rack lies across the bay as a 1.4 m high ridge of H-section with its containers around it: new cover, new slopes, a Slingshot surface. The bay's canyon is now a crossing. The B-Low catwalk is gone on that side. |

What breaks: the rack's remaining bolts, one B-Low catwalk section, the containers' doors, anything underneath. Reset between rounds.

## Interactions

- **Haul** the uphill post across the fall axis: the commit. From the far side of the bay the Breaker is 7 m away, which is inside the fall arc's end (8.2 m). Brace, and step back during the groan; the lead time is for you too.
- **Jack** between the rack and an enemy: Overboard across the bay into the shaft lip.
- **Hang** from the rack: a Hang line never unseats it (05 section 6.2); any class Gallows-anchors to the top beam up to the class Hang load. Hang an enemy's back plate to the rack's top beam before committing: they are hoisted as the beam falls, then dragged under it. The Gallows into the Avalanche.
- Chain: fuel drums spilled along the fall path land at 9 m/s: over the 10 m/s drum trigger only if they fall from the top container (11 m/s). Expect one blast per loaded-rack fall, and a fire patch on the ridge.
