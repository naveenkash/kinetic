# Evidence Objects

One-line: environment, six Light props (2 to 14 kg), one per planned season (Claims 62 to 67), each a physical piece of the Question (01) recoverable by any player from an authored location in one arena and logged to the Ledger (04). Pickup is a 2.0 s hold (**PROPOSED**), interruptible, once per player per season.

## Role in play

Evidence objects are how the story is found in the wreck. Each season one appears in one arena at a place that costs something to reach. Any player can collect it (a hold, not a Dog), which writes a Ledger entry with that faction's reading. Collecting during a match is legal and slightly stupid: two seconds standing still in a place the designers chose for its exposure. The Office's position is that the evidence is undetermined; the Office also places it where the cameras can see.

Rules for all six: Light prop, Dogs seat in it (a line can move it from its spot, and it can be Hauled into a Well, where it resets at round end like the Claim marker), not destructible, not volatile, a yellow Office impound tag wired to it. A collected object is removed from that player's world until the next match; other players still see it. Its position is fixed per season; it does not reset within a match if moved, only between rounds.

## Appearance

Each object shares a kit: an Office impound tag (yellow card, 100 mm x 150 mm, wired on, with the Claim number and `EVIDENCE: DO NOT REMOVE` in black), a 0.1 m emissive Office marker light (Bone #E8E6E0, 10 lm, visible at 30 m, never brighter than a line) clipped beside it, and a chalk circle from the Guild around its position. The object itself is in the material language of its origin: Consortium orange-and-black for the recorder, Guild ochre for the log, cracked glass and a Collar Three frame for the waiver, nitrided steel and a Weave face for the grip dog, LayCast black and a crude Fray weld for the core, the Weave sheen for the edge.

## Physics and interaction

- Light prop. Mass per table. Racked at 200 N (wired to its tag point): any line frees it; it then travels like any Light prop (the recorder at 14 kg on a Scrapper's 400 N is 29 m/s², the cap in 11 m; 350 N s, a stagger). Not destructible. Into a Well: resets at round end.
- Pickup: 2.0 s hold on the interact input within 1.5 m, uninterrupted by stagger (stagger locks movement and Spool; interact is treated as a Cutter-class input and stays live, **PROPOSED**) but interrupted by moving, being hoisted, or being flipped. On completion: Ledger entry, a 2 s Face bark from the collector's Allegiance, and a LayCast lower-third on the broadcast feed ("Evidence recovered") visible to all players. The object remains for others.
- Placement costs, by design: 62 is in x3.0 water beside a Well with a 90 s tilt cycle; 63 is in the Rails' most exposed room or in a ruin in the open; 64 is inside a dynamic house; 65 is on the Crown under the Claim marker; 66 is under a stall that drops to 40 degrees at the event; 67 is 3 m from the Yard's Well on the unrailed lip.

## States

`Placed` (marker on), `Moved` (marker follows the object), `Collected (local)` (hidden for that player), `Reset`.

## Technical

- One actor class `BP_EvidenceObject` with a per-season data asset: mesh, mass, arena, location socket, Ledger entry IDs per faction, bark IDs. LOD0 under 2,000 tris each, LOD1 half, marker light persists to LOD3.
- Textures: `T_Evidence_<claim>` 2K each; shared `T_Office_Tag` 512.
- Sockets: `Dog_Seat`, `Tag`, `Marker`, `Interact` (1.5 m sphere). Location sockets on host actors: `Evidence_Spawn` on the Hold sump, the control shack desk and the shack ruin, the administrator's house wall, the Crown center, the Fray stall counter, the broken-strap crate.
- Replication: object transform replicated as a Light prop; collection state is per player (server-validated hold, client-predicted progress). Season gating by a live config value `CurrentClaim`.
- Collision: `Kinetic_Prop`; the interact sphere is `Kinetic_Interact`.
